# Glossary — Japanese (Malaysian usage)

Agreed Japanese renderings for recurring NegaraKu.md terms, so translators render
them consistently. Japanese here describes **Malaysia** for a Japanese-reading
audience (investors, professionals, residents, tourists) — use the terms Japanese
business/press actually use for Malaysian institutions, transliterating local
names rather than inventing new ones.

This file is seeded and grows as translators coin terms. Read
`docs/TRANSLATION-SPEC.md` first — this glossary only settles the recurring
renderings; the spec's rules (never-translate list, first-use pattern, faithful
translation) always apply.

## Never translate — keep in the original script

Statute short titles (`Companies Act 2016`, `Akta 777`), gazette citations
(`P.U.(A) 445/2017`), section/paragraph refs (`s.7(f)`), Schedules/Parts (gloss
once, keep English), agency acronyms (SSM, LHDN, RMCD, MDEC, MIDA, MITI, DOSM,
EPF, SOCSO/PERKESO, BNM, SC, MCMC, AKPK, HRD), form/scheme names (`Form E`,
`MD Status`, `LMW`, `DMP`), URLs, and company/place names in local form
(`Sdn Bhd`, `Bursa Malaysia`, `Ipoh`, `Cyberjaya`).

## First use of an institution

Give the Japanese term, then the original English/acronym in brackets, then the
short form after: e.g. `企業委員会（SSM）` … then `SSM`.

## Numbers, currency, dates

- Currency: `RM` / `リンギット` (ringgit); keep the `RM` symbol with figures
  (e.g. `RM1,000`), gloss as リンギット on first use.
- Use Japanese full-width punctuation（、。）in prose; keep ASCII inside code,
  citations, URLs and statute refs.
- Dates: render as `2026年9月18日` in prose; keep ISO form in citations.

## Settled conventions (from the Phase 3 accounting batch)

- **Section / paragraph references stay abbreviated and inline** — `s.245(2)`, `s.68`, `para 5`, `First Schedule`. Do NOT convert to `第245条` / `第5項`; keep the `s.` form embedded directly in the Japanese sentence (matches the spec's never-translate list and the `.ja` pilot: companies-act-2016, digital-free-trade-zone).
- **Statute titles:** Japanese descriptor + canonical English name/number in brackets, Japanese-comma separator — `2016年会社法（Companies Act 2016、Act 777）`. Standard/PD titles may use `『…』` for the work title.
- **Money scale words** (bilion/juta/million/billion) → Japanese `億/万`, exact value preserved; digit-form figures (`RM500,000`, `10%`) unchanged.

**Money scale (agriculture reaffirms the locked rule):** 1 bilion = 10億, 1 juta =
100万, 1 trilion = 1兆 — convert word-form scale figures to 億/万/兆 preserving the
EXACT value (`RM103.2 bilion` → `RM1,032億`, `5.65 juta hektar` → `565万ヘクタール`).
Digit-form figures (`RM100,000`, `980,000 tan`, `40.46`, percentages) stay
digit-form — do NOT regroup them into 万 (that's a zh-sibling artifact, not the JA rule).
