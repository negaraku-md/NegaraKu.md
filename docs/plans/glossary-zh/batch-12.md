# Batch 12 — coined terms (company-secretary, 14 articles)

Terms not in `GLOSSARY-ZH.md` that had to be decided here. Used consistently
across all 14 files in this batch.

| English | Chinese | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| beneficial owner / beneficial ownership | 受益所有人／受益所有权 | Matches SSM's own Malay-Chinese press usage. Rejected 实益拥有人 (HK usage) |
| register of beneficial owners (RBO) | 受益所有人登记册 | Built on the glossary's existing 法定登记册 pattern |
| lodgement (with the Registrar) | 提交 | Kept distinct from 传阅 (circulation) and 呈报 (filing generally) — the s.258/s.259 distinction is the whole point of two articles |

| Registrar (of Companies) | 注册官 | Not 注册处 — the Act addresses a person, and s.259(2) turns on his discretion |
| rectification (s.602) vs update | 更正／更新 | Two e-BOS operations the master insists must not be conflated |
