## Notes on house-style application

- The LHDN e-Invoice Guideline blockquote in `e-invoice-accounting-records` was **left
  in English verbatim**, per the ruling that literally-quoted English source text keeps
  `"` and its original wording. Flagged for reviewer: a Chinese gloss beneath it may be
  wanted.
- `conveniently and properly audited` (s.245(1)(b)) and `sufficiently explain`
  (s.245(1)(a)) are rendered identically everywhere they appear across
  `accounting-records-section-245`, `e-invoice-accounting-records` and
  `director-right-to-inspect-records`, because three articles argue from the same phrase.
- `circulate`/`lodge` are never collapsed into one verb. Four articles depend on the
  reader seeing two sequential clocks.
