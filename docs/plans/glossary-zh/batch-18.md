# Batch 18 — coined terms (taxation, 15 articles)

Terms not in `GLOSSARY-ZH.md` at the time of translation. Chosen once and used
consistently across the batch.

| English | Chinese | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| advance pricing arrangement (APA) | 预先定价安排（APA） | Then `APA` alone. 预约定价 is the mainland term; 预先定价 reads more naturally against the Malay *pengaturan penetapan harga terlebih dahulu* |
| transfer pricing | 转让定价 | Settled term; recorded here for consistency |
| covered period / covered transaction | 涵盖期／涵盖交易 | Rejected 适用期 — the Rules use "covered" as a defined scope, not "applicable" |
| rollback (of an APA) | 追溯适用 | Rejected 回溯 (reads as reversal). The mechanism applies agreed terms backwards |
| competent authority | 主管当局 | Treaty term of art. Distinct from 管理当局 (zone Authority, already in the glossary) |
| Director General (LHDN) | 总监 | s.138C etc. First use glossed; thereafter bare |
| critical assumptions | 关键假设 | rule 4(2) item |

| Special Commissioners | Special Commissioners | Left in English — no settled Malaysian Chinese rendering; the body is always cited by name |
