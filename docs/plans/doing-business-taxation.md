## Differentiation

1. **Nobody routes between regimes.** Every competitor writes one tax per page. Our
   routing articles (`capital-or-revenue`, `e-invoice-vs-tax-invoice`,
   `withholding-tax-digital-services`) are pure whitespace.
2. **Consequence is missing.** Competitors state rules but not what non-compliance
   costs — the s.39(1)(j) disallowance stacked on the 10% WHT increase, the
   s.140A(3C) surcharge with no additional tax, the CP204 variance penalty. These
   are the 11pm questions, buried in Big 4 PDFs.
3. **Written for the wrong reader.** Big 4 targets in-scope MNEs; SME blogs target
   lead capture. TP, Pillar Two and FSI substance now bite mid-market companies who
   have nothing written for them.
4. **Nothing is maintained as data.** Every rate table on the web is prose in a blog
   post of unknown date. Our T4 pages are structured, dated and citable — which is
   also what makes the corpus useful to AI agents.
5. **Labuan is a hole.** Firms that sell it write promotional content; firms that
   don't, ignore it.

## Recon notes

Three tiers rank. Official (hasil.gov.my, mysst.customs.gov.my) wins navigational
queries, rarely informational. Big 4 and mid-tier (PwC WWTS, Crowe, EY, KPMG, BDO,
Grant Thornton, RSM) win technical queries — authoritative but published as dated
alerts, often PDFs. The commercial long tail (ClearTax MY, 3E, Acclime, YYC, L&Co,
KTP, ringgitplus, PropertyGuru) dominates high-volume consumer queries — thin and
heavily year-stamped. ClearTax MY is the most consistent generalist competitor.

**Winning format by query type.** Consumer queries → long listicles with a table and
a year in the title. Compliance queries → phase/timeline tables (every top MyInvois
result is one). Technical queries → firm alerts and PDFs, beatable on structure.

**Year convention.** `<Topic> Malaysia 2026` is near-universal; where YA differs from
filing year the top results carry both: "Personal Income Tax Guide 2026 (YA 2025)".
Adopt: year in H1, YA in parentheses where it differs, **slug stays year-free**.
