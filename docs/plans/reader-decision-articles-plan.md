## Thesis
Build articles around **specific reader decisions and problems**, on top of the broad guides already
live — move the reader from "I understand the topic" to "I know what to check or do next." This is the
same decision-first philosophy as the stayFacts cards; here it is whole supporting articles that link
existing reference material at the moment of a decision. Publish as **connected clusters**, and
**expand/restructure an existing article rather than create a new page when the question is already
answered well enough.**

## Briefs (3 worked examples)
- **College deposit** — Q: "What must we verify before paying?" Opening: concise checklist of independent checks. Content: exact programme/intake; recognition; complete costs; progression; refund terms. Output: questions to send the institution. Evidence: MQA, relevant professional bodies, institution's current written terms. Scope limit: no promised admission/exemption without confirmation.
- **Business premises lease** — Q: "Can this business legally and practically operate here?" Opening: why registration and premises suitability are separate checks. Content: business activity; premises use; relevant authorities; approvals; lease protections. Output: questions for landlord/council/advisers. Evidence: relevant council + sector authorities, applicable instruments. Scope limit: no claim one checklist covers every council/activity.
- **MP, ADUN or council** — Q: "Who can deal with this problem?" Opening: clarify responsibility/representation/enforcement. Content: common scenarios; responsible bodies; evidence to submit; escalation. Output: problem-to-authority lookup table. Evidence: official functions, service channels, jurisdiction. Scope limit: do not imply an elected rep controls an agency.
