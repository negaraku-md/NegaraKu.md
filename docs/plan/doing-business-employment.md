## Differentiation

1. **Obligations as structured data.** Nobody in this market publishes deadlines
   machine-readably. The Compliance Calendar is a moat, not a feature.
2. **Currency as a discipline.** Half the top-ranking pages carry superseded
   thresholds. Dated figures with per-figure citation beats everything ranking now.
3. **Sabah and Sarawak.** Systematically ignored. Two Ordinances, two JTK departments.
4. **The neglected statutes.** Act 446, s.28 Contracts Act, TBLB Regulations 1980,
   forced labour, Act 872.
5. **Trilingual.** Competitors are English-only or English-first; JTKSM/PERKESO primary
   material is BM. Chinese-language MY employer content is thin to nonexistent.
6. **Procedure, not just entitlement.** Domestic inquiry mechanics, Form PK
   part-by-part, s.20 conciliation flow — steps law-firm posts assume and HR blogs omit.

## Recon notes

**ajobthing** runs highest-volume news-driven HR posts — well-indexed, frequently stale
(still states the pre-2023 RM2,000 coverage rule). **3ecpa** has thin evergreen pages
with visibly outdated figures (RM4,000 SOCSO cap, superseded Oct 2024). **mishu** covers
payroll at cosec altitude, dated 2023. **quickhr / swingvy / omni / payrollcalculator.my**
dominate rate-table SERPs with SaaS lead-gen. Official sources (kwsp, perkeso, jtksm,
esd.imi, hrdcorp) are authoritative but siloed, poorly cross-linked and largely BM-first.

**Winning format:** rate/deadline queries → a table above the fold plus "2026" in the
title. Law queries → law-firm long-form (Donovan & Ho, MahWengKwai, LHAG, Herbert
Smith) which wins on quality but is written for lawyers and never updated.

**Established by recon (none citable):** Employment (Amendment) Act 2022 in force
1 Jan 2023, scope extended to all employees with specified provisions restricted above
RM4,000; week 48 → 45 hours; maternity 60 → 98 days; paternity 7 days/5 confinements;
FWA 60-day response; Part VIII repealed; s.60K prior approval; forced-labour offence.
Minimum wage RM1,700 effective Feb 2025 under the 2024 Order, no 2026 Order surfaced.
EPF non-citizen 2% from the Oct 2025 wage month. SOCSO/EIS ceiling RM6,000 since Oct
2024. EP revision effective 1 Jun 2026. MTLM not yet gazetted. Act 872 in force
31 Mar 2026, platform SOCSO deduction reported at 1.25%. OSHA amendment in force
1 Jun 2024, OSH Coordinator at 5+. Form PK: Parts I–IV ≥30 days before, Part V within
14 days after, Part VI within 30 days after.

  distributing unreviewed legal templates carries liability we shouldn't take on.
