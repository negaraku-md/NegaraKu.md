# Batch 15 — coined terms (employment, 15 articles)

Terms decided while translating batch 15. Not added to `GLOSSARY-ZH.md` directly.

| English | Chinese | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| domestic inquiry (DI) | 内部调查 | Malaysian HR practice term. Gloss `内部调查（domestic inquiry，DI）` on first use. Rejected 内部聆讯 — the Act's own word is *inquiry*, and 聆讯 collides with the hearing step inside it |
| Industrial Court | 劳资法庭 | Established Malaysian Chinese press term; not mainland 劳动仲裁委员会, not 工业法庭 |
| Labour Court | 劳工法庭 | Kept distinct from 劳资法庭 (Industrial Court) — the two are different forums and several articles name both |
| Director General (of Labour) | 劳工总监 | Then 总监 alone. Matches JTKSM's own Chinese-language usage |
| show-cause letter | 要求解释信 | Tracks Malay *surat tunjuk sebab*. Rejected bare 解释信, which reads as the reply rather than the demand |
