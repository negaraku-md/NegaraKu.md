# Glossary — Bahasa Malaysia termbase

The agreed Malay rendering for recurring terms, so every MS translation (and every
MS-master article) stays consistent. Mirrors `GLOSSARY-ZH.md` for the Chinese side.
Add to it as translation proceeds; when a term is contested, record the ruling and
the reason.

## Rules of the road

- **Keep in the original script** (do not translate): agency acronyms (SSM, LHDN,
  RMCD, EPF, KWSP, SOCSO/PERKESO, BNM, SC, MDEC, MIDA, MITI, DOSM); Act names may be
  given in Malay (Akta …) with the English in a clause on first use; statute section
  refs (`s.7(f)`, `para 2B`); money (`RM2,236`), percentages, dates.
- **Malaysian BM usage**, not Indonesian. `syarikat` not `perusahaan`; `pengarah`
  not `direktur`; `cukai` not `pajak`; `mesyuarat` not `rapat`.
- English loan-terms that Malaysian BM uses as-is stay as-is (e.g. *audit*).

## Rulings

- *Sdn Bhd* and agency acronyms are kept verbatim — they are identifiers, not prose.
- Act names: give the Malay form (`Akta Syarikat 2016`) with the English once in a
  clause on first use, then use the Malay form.
