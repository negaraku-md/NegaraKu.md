# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
NegaraKu.md a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Given NegaraKu.md documents a diverse, multi-ethnic, multi-faith nation, we hold
an especially high standard for respectful, sourced, and neutral discussion of
sensitive topics (race, religion, the monarchy, politics).

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the accuracy of the content.
- Citing sources and separating fact from opinion.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Content that incites hostility between communities.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
