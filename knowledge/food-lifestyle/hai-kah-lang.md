In a shoplot in Taman Cheras, a residential pocket of south-eastern Kuala
Lumpur, Hai Kah Lang does one thing and does it to order: a bowl of seafood
noodles built exactly the way you want it.

## What it is, and the Bib Gourmand

Hai Kah Lang is a seafood specialist recognised in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang with a **Bib Gourmand**, the guide's mark of good quality at good
value. The inspectors describe a place "always packed with customers craving
seafood noodles," and credit its quality to a simple advantage: the owner also
runs a seafood stall nearby, so only the freshest catch reaches the table. It
sits in the $$ price band.

## How to order

The format is build-your-own. You pick your favourite **fish, shellfish or a
combination of both**, then choose from **eight types of noodles and five soup
bases**. If that many permutations feel overwhelming, the MICHELIN Guide points
first-timers to two house favourites: the **mixed seafood noodles** and the
**fish head noodle soup with milk** — a rich, pale broth that is something of a
signature. The restaurant has even shared its clear fish-broth recipe with the
guide, a nod to how central the soup is to its reputation.

## Where to find it

Hai Kah Lang is at 44 Jalan Kaskas 2, Taman Cheras, Kuala Lumpur 56100, and
keeps daytime hours — roughly 8am to 5pm — so it suits a breakfast or lunch visit
rather than a late dinner. It is air-conditioned and accepts credit cards, and is
one of the Kuala Lumpur entries in the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## Visiting note

Come hungry and come earlier in the day, given the daytime hours and the crowds
the guide describes. As with every Bib Gourmand, the award is reviewed each year,
so check the current MICHELIN listing before a dedicated trip — and if the menu's
many combinations stall you at the counter, one of the two house favourites is
the safe way in.
