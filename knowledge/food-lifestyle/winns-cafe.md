Penang is the old heartland of Straits-Chinese cooking, and Winn's Cafe keeps that
tradition in the most personal way possible — one family's Nyonya recipes, cooked
in a George Town house and now recognised by the MICHELIN Guide.

## A Bib Gourmand Peranakan kitchen

Winn's Cafe is listed as a **MICHELIN Bib Gourmand** in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang selection, the award for good food at good value. Its kitchen is
devoted to [Peranakan, or Nyonya, cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine)
— the dishes of the Straits-born Chinese communities whose blend of Chinese
ingredients and Malay spice pastes shaped much of how Penang eats.

## Signature dishes

MICHELIN singles out the **otak otak**, a fish cake with a notably smooth texture,
seasoned with coconut milk and herbs grown in the restaurant's own backyard, and
the **asam tumis stingray**, whose tender meat comes in a rich sauce built from
nine herbs. The guide's seafood coverage also praises its big prawns — a reminder
that this is home-style Nyonya cooking made with care rather than shortcuts.

## Setting and background

A husband-and-wife team runs Winn's Cafe from a **detached house at 38 Jalan
Cantonment, George Town, 10350**, with dining rooms spread over two floors and the
upper level given to work by local artists. MICHELIN notes the division of labour
that gives the place its character: the wife cooks using her family's Nyonya
recipes while the husband looks after the guests.

## Visiting

Unlike Penang's many hawker stalls, this is a sit-down restaurant with air
conditioning that accepts credit cards. MICHELIN lists lunch and dinner service
from Monday to Saturday, closed on Sunday; as hours can change, it is worth
confirming — and, given the home-kitchen scale, calling ahead before a special
visit.
