Gulainya is a Peranakan restaurant in Bukit Damansara, Kuala Lumpur, that
appears in the [MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
with a Bib Gourmand. MICHELIN's ceremony coverage presents it as one of the
newest additions to the Malaysia Bib Gourmand selection.

## A new Bib Gourmand for Peranakan home cooking

The MICHELIN Guide lists Gulainya with a **Bib Gourmand**, its award for good
quality at good value — separate from the one-to-three-star ratings. MICHELIN's
Bib Gourmand announcement names it among the latest additions in Kuala Lumpur,
alongside the restaurant Lama. As with every Bib Gourmand entry, the recognition
is reviewed each year.

## The food

According to the MICHELIN Guide, Gulainya stands out for the owner's family
recipes, "polished and updated," with the sauces made by the owner himself and
an emphasis on fresh local produce and home-style dishes. The Guide's
highlighted plate is a creamy basil boneless chicken, described as tender in a
herb-forward cream sauce. For the wider tradition behind this cooking, see
[Nyonya and Peranakan cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine).

## Where to find it

The MICHELIN Guide gives the address as 44-G, Plaza Damansara, Jalan Medan Setia
2, Bukit Damansara, 50490 Kuala Lumpur, and places it in the $$ price band. The
listing notes alfresco dining and that the restaurant is closed on Mondays.

## Visiting note

Gulainya is a neighbourhood restaurant whose hours, menu and prices can change,
and little of its background is publicly documented. Confirm current details
before visiting, and treat the Bib Gourmand listing — revised annually — as
current rather than permanent.
