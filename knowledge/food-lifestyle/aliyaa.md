Aliyaa is a Sri Lankan restaurant in Bukit Damansara, Kuala Lumpur, and one of
the comparatively few places in the city devoted to that cuisine. It appears in
the [MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) with a
Bib Gourmand.

## A Bib Gourmand for Sri Lankan cooking

The MICHELIN Guide lists Aliyaa with a **Bib Gourmand**, its award for good
quality at good value — separate from the one-to-three-star ratings. The Guide
describes the restaurant as famous for authentic Sri Lankan dishes, set among
the bars and eateries of Plaza Damansara. As with every Bib Gourmand entry, the
recognition is reviewed each year.

## The food

The MICHELIN Guide says Aliyaa's menu runs across beef, mutton, chicken, seafood
and vegetarian dishes, seasoned with distinctive Sri Lankan spices, and that the
cooking is generally spicy — though the kitchen will adjust the heat to a
diner's liking. Among the dishes the Guide highlights are a whole crab, Negombo
prawns in pineapple gravy, and kuliyal (described as curry rice and tomato paste
baked in banana leaf). The whole crab in particular has drawn repeated mention,
including in MICHELIN's round-up of seafood dining in Malaysia.

## Where to find it

The MICHELIN Guide gives the address as 48 Jalan Medan Setia 2, Bukit Damansara
(Plaza Damansara), 50490 Kuala Lumpur, and places it in the $$ price band. The
listing notes air conditioning, counter dining and a terrace.

## Visiting note

Aliyaa is a neighbourhood restaurant whose hours, menu and reservation policy
can change. Confirm current details before visiting, and treat the Bib Gourmand
listing — revised annually — as current rather than permanent.
