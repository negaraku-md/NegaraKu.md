Tucked into the coffee-shop bustle of [Joo Hooi Cafe](/food-lifestyle/penang-food)
on Jalan Penang, this stall is one of George Town's most talked-about plates of
char kuey teow — and it carries a MICHELIN Guide stamp to match the queues.

## What it is

Penang Road Famous Jin Kor Char Kuey Teow is a hawker stall, not a sit-down
restaurant, operating from a corner of Joo Hooi Cafe. The MICHELIN Guide lists it
as a **Bib Gourmand** pick — the Guide's distinction for good-quality,
good-value cooking, awarded to casual and street-food spots as readily as to
restaurants (see the [MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
for how the distinctions work). It is a street-food listing in the Guide's lowest
price band, cash-only, and does not take reservations.

## The signature plate

The draw is a single dish: [char kuey teow](/food-lifestyle/char-kway-teow), the
flat rice noodles stir-fried hard over high heat. The MICHELIN listing says each
plate is made to order and topped with prawns, blood cockles, Chinese sausage and
bean sprouts, with a "lovely wok hei" — the smoky, slightly charred aroma that a
roaring wok imparts and that Penang diners prize above all else. The Guide
suggests adding a duck egg and extra chilli sauce for a richer, spicier plate.

## Where to find it

The stall operates inside Joo Hooi Cafe at 475 Jalan Penang, George Town, 10000,
Penang. Per the MICHELIN listing it opens daily from 09:30 to 17:00, though a
hawker stall's hours shift with the cook, so it is worth confirming locally. It
sits in the heart of the old town, within walking distance of many of Penang's
other street-food landmarks.

## Before you go

Bring cash — credit cards are not accepted — and expect to wait at busy times, as
the noodles are fried in small batches to order. There are no bookings, so you
simply join the queue. As with any hawker listing, the MICHELIN selection is
reviewed each year, so check the current Guide if the distinction is what brings
you.
