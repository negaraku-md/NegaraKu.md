De. Wan 1958 is a Malaysian restaurant at The LINC KL in Taman U Thant, Kuala
Lumpur, associated with the Malaysian celebrity chef known as Chef Wan. It
appears in the [MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
with a Bib Gourmand.

## A Bib Gourmand from a celebrity chef

The MICHELIN Guide lists De. Wan 1958 with a **Bib Gourmand**, its award for
good quality at good value — separate from the one-to-three-star ratings. The
Guide likens the restaurant's bright, cheerful room to the mood of Chef Wan
himself. By widely published accounts the "1958" in the name is the chef's birth
year, though that detail is best confirmed against a biography. As with every
Bib Gourmand entry, the recognition is reviewed each year.

## The food

The MICHELIN Guide describes bold tropical flavours and recommends opening with
a pomelo salad with crispy prawn cheeks in a kerabu lime dressing lifted with a
touch of fish sauce. For mains it points to **bakar bakar** — barbecued meat or
seafood served with a choice of the chef's sauces — and notes that portions are
generous and suited to sharing. The cooking sits within Malaysia's broad
[Malay culinary tradition](/food-lifestyle/malay-cuisine).

## Where to find it

The MICHELIN Guide gives the address as Level 2-2, The LINC KL, 360 Jalan Tun
Razak, Taman U Thant, 50400 Kuala Lumpur, and places it in the $$ price band,
noting it is good for groups.

## Visiting note

Opening hours, menu and prices can change. Confirm current details before
visiting, and treat the Bib Gourmand listing — revised annually — as current
rather than permanent.
