Most of Penang's MICHELIN attention falls on George Town, across the water. BM
Yam Rice is a reminder that the mainland — Seberang Perai, and the market town of
Bukit Mertajam in particular — has its own deep food culture, and here a modest
Teochew shop has earned a place in the Guide.

## What it is

BM Yam Rice is a small, family-run Teochew eatery recognised in the MICHELIN
Guide as a **Bib Gourmand** pick — the Guide's distinction for good-quality,
good-value cooking (see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) for how the
distinctions work). It is one of relatively few Seberang Perai listings in a
selection otherwise centred on George Town. Its listing status has moved between
MICHELIN Selected and Bib Gourmand across editions, so the current Guide is the
place to confirm it.

## The signature dish

The house specialty is yam rice — rice cooked with taro so the grains turn
savoury and fragrant — paired with a pork soup. The MICHELIN listing says the yam
rice is "nicely cooked, with strong shrimp flavour," while the pork broth is
"briny-sweet" and lifted by pickled mustard greens that give it a tangy depth.
Diners choose whether to have pork offal in the soup; the Guide notes the offal
is cooked to the right texture and the pork balls are peppery and flavoursome.
Everything follows the owner's family recipe, served since the shop opened.

## Where to find it

The shop is at 7 Jalan Murthy, Bukit Mertajam, Seberang Perai, 14000, Penang. The
MICHELIN listing shows it trading from 09:00 to about 15:00 on most days and
closed on Wednesday, though the Guide's regional pages give slightly different
schedules — a reminder to confirm locally.

## Before you go

The shop has served its family recipe since opening its doors in 2006. It is a
small, casual place, so go early for the freshest soup and expect a simple,
no-frills setting. As with every MICHELIN listing, the selection is reviewed each
year, so check the current Guide if the distinction is what draws you across to
the mainland.
