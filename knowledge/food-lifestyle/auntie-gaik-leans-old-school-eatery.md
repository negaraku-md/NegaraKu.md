Auntie Gaik Lean's Old School Eatery is a one-MICHELIN-star restaurant in George
Town, Penang, serving Peranakan (Nyonya) cuisine from closely guarded family
recipes. It is run by its self-taught chef and namesake, Beh Gaik Lean.

## What it is

The eatery is at 1 Lebuh Bishop in the heart of George Town. The MICHELIN Guide
classifies it as [Peranakan](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine) — the
cooking of the Straits-born Chinese, or Nyonya, community — and lists it at an
accessible `$$` price level, unusually modest for a starred restaurant. In the
Guide's description, "memorabilia in show windows and a 60s soundtrack exude the
charm of a bygone era," and many dishes are made from scratch. It is one of
Penang's two MICHELIN-starred restaurants and a landmark of
[George Town's food culture](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## MICHELIN recognition

The restaurant was among those awarded a MICHELIN star in the inaugural
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia),
announced in December 2022, for what the Guide calls "Peranakan cuisine crafted
from secret recipes using quality produce." It has retained one star through the
2026 selection. MICHELIN reassesses its selections every year, so the
distinction reflects the current edition.

## The food

The kitchen specialises in heritage Nyonya dishes. The MICHELIN Guide singles
out a "heavenly" pie tee (crisp top-hat pastry cups with a vegetable filling), a
*gulai tumis* whose curry paste the Guide says uses more than eight ingredients,
and *nasi ulam*, described as an old-time recipe. The cooking leans on
labour-intensive, from-scratch preparation rather than shortcuts — the
through-line of the broader
[Nyonya and Peranakan tradition](/arts-culture/peranakan).

## The chef

The restaurant is named for **Beh Gaik Lean**, a self-taught, fourth-generation
Nyonya cook from Penang who learned the cuisine within her own family. By
widely reported accounts she received the MICHELIN star in her late sixties and
is cited as among the first Malaysian women to be so recognised. The eatery
opened in 2013, and she has guarded its recipes closely for decades.

## Visiting

The MICHELIN Guide lists Auntie Gaik Lean's as open Wednesday to Sunday for
lunch and dinner and closed Monday and Tuesday, with air conditioning and card
payment, and bookings handled by the restaurant directly. As a small,
much-sought restaurant whose hours and distinction are reviewed over time, it is
worth confirming the current opening times and reservation arrangements with the
restaurant before planning a visit.
