Beta is a modern Malaysian fine-dining restaurant on Jalan Perak in central
Kuala Lumpur. It holds One MICHELIN Star in the
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur & Penang,
where its listing describes a tasting menu that reimagines the country's dishes
through modern technique and elaborate presentation.

## A one-MICHELIN-star restaurant

Beta holds **One MICHELIN Star**, the guide's distinction for "high-quality
cooking" that is "worth a stop," and appears in MICHELIN's current full
selection for Malaysia. Secondary reporting records that Beta was a MICHELIN
Selected restaurant in the inaugural Malaysian guide before being promoted to
one star, which it has since retained. The star places it among a small group of
restaurants that define contemporary [fine dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia)
in Kuala Lumpur.

## The food: a 'Tour of Malaysia'

The MICHELIN Guide classifies Beta's cuisine as **Malaysian and Contemporary**.
Its listing explains that the chef "adds drama to local favourites" with
"modern techniques and meticulous plating," and that the restaurant's "Tour of
Malaysia" menu "celebrates the breadth and depth of the country's culinary
heritage." MICHELIN singles out an abalone Lawas rice add-on — described as
creamy Sarawakian heirloom rice topped with abalone cubes — and notes that
cocktail and wine pairings are available. The approach is a refined take on the
dishes that sit at the heart of [Malaysia's national table](/food-lifestyle/national-dishes-of-malaysia).

## Setting and location

Beta occupies Lot 3 & 4 on the ground floor of Cormar Suites, 10 Jalan Perak,
Kuala Lumpur. The MICHELIN Guide calls the dining room "quite stunning" and
"theatrical," and lists facilities including air conditioning, car park and
valet parking. The guide flags the restaurant as good for a date night.

## Visiting Beta

Per its MICHELIN listing, Beta serves dinner Tuesday to Sunday and is closed on
Mondays. The guide places it in the `$$$` price band. As a fine-dining room
built around a tasting menu, it is best visited with a reservation booked ahead.
As with every MICHELIN entry, the star reflects the guide's most recent
published selection and is reviewed each year.
