Hor Poh Cuisine is an unassuming Hakka restaurant in the Segambut district of
Kuala Lumpur, and one of the Hakka specialists recognised in the
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur & Penang
selection.

## What it is

The shop carries a **Bib Gourmand**, the MICHELIN distinction for good food at
good value rather than a MICHELIN Star. The Guide is plain about the setting:
"this simple shop has been selling authentic Hakkanese food in an unremarkable
neighbourhood for over 20 years," offering a range of tasty dishes at bargain
prices to a crowd that keeps it busy. It is filed in the lowest ($) price tier.

## The signature dishes

The must-try is **lei cha**, the Hakka dish often translated as *thunder tea
rice*. At Hor Poh it arrives as a bright green soup — ground from sesame seeds,
peanuts and mint, and described by MICHELIN as nutty and aromatic — served
alongside steamed rice. MICHELIN's Hakka-food feature notes that the restaurant's
version "goes beyond the classic mix," adding crispy fried beancurd skin for
crunch and braised black-eyed peas for texture. The kitchen's other standout is
its **Hakka dumplings**, filled with dried tofu, garlic and pickles in a thin
skin.

## Location

Hor Poh Cuisine is at **36 Jalan 6/38D, Taman Sri Sinar, Segambut, Kuala Lumpur
52100**, in a residential quarter on the city's north-western side. It is a small
shop; the MICHELIN Guide notes air conditioning, that bookings are handled
directly, and that the place is usually busy.

## Hakka food in Malaysia

Hakka cooking is one of the enduring strands of Malaysian Chinese cuisine, and
dishes like lei cha and the community's distinctive dumplings carry a frugal,
herb-forward character that sets them apart from other regional styles. A shop
like Hor Poh, keeping those recipes alive in an ordinary neighbourhood, is
exactly the kind of place the Bib Gourmand was created to reward.

## Visiting note

Because it is small and popular, Hor Poh fills up quickly, and specialties such
as the lei cha can run out. It is worth arriving early and confirming opening
hours before a special trip — the shop closes on Tuesdays — and treating the
MICHELIN listing as the authoritative record of its current status.
