Anak Baba is a Peranakan eatery in Brickfields — Kuala Lumpur's Little India —
that appears in the [MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
with a Bib Gourmand. The MICHELIN Guide calls it a "cosy little gem."

## A Bib Gourmand for Peranakan home cooking

The MICHELIN Guide lists Anak Baba with a **Bib Gourmand**, its award for good
quality at good value — separate from the one-to-three-star ratings. The Guide
places it in the lowest price band ($), the band typical of the unpretentious
local kitchens the Bib Gourmand is designed to recognise. As with every Bib
Gourmand entry, the recognition is reviewed each year.

## Southern Peranakan food

According to the MICHELIN Guide, Anak Baba has served Southern Peranakan food —
cooking that blends Indo-Malay flavours with Chinese influences — since 2018.
The Guide's highlighted dish is the nasi lemak with ayam goreng kunyit, pairing
coconut-milk rice with turmeric fried chicken, sambal and crisp water spinach.
For the wider tradition behind this cooking, see
[Nyonya and Peranakan cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine).

## Where to find it

The MICHELIN Guide gives the address as 159 Jalan Sultan Abdul Samad,
Brickfields, 50470 Kuala Lumpur — tucked inside the Little India quarter southwest
of the city centre.

## Visiting note

Anak Baba is a small, casual eatery, so its hours, prices and menu can change.
Confirm current details before visiting, and treat the Bib Gourmand listing —
revised annually — as current rather than permanent.
