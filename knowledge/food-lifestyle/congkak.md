On a quiet residential street in Bukit Bintang, a few turns off Kuala Lumpur's
busiest shopping district, Congkak serves the kind of Malaysian food most
visitors never see on a hotel menu — the dishes locals grew up eating at home.

## What it is, and the Bib Gourmand

Congkak is a Malaysian restaurant recognised in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur
& Penang with a **Bib Gourmand** — the guide's distinction for good quality
cooking at good value, awarded separately from its Stars. The inspectors describe
the place as "an expression of national pride and identity," serving dishes that
a first-time visitor might find exotic but that are everyday classics for
Malaysians. It sits in the $$ (moderate) price band and carries the guide's "Eat
like a local" tag.

## The signature: nasi ambeng

The dish most associated with Congkak is its **nasi ambeng** platter — a
communal spread built around rice and a range of accompaniments, meant to be
shared. The MICHELIN Guide singles it out as the most popular order, praising its
mix of textures and its balance of spicy, sweet and savoury flavours, and notes
that portion sizes vary to suit the size of your party. It belongs to Malaysia's
wider family of
[rice-based dishes](/food-lifestyle/malaysian-rice-dishes), where a plate of rice
anchors a cluster of sides rather than standing alone.

## Where to find it

Congkak is at 24 Jalan Beremi, Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur 50200 — on a
residential street close to the central district rather than inside a mall. It
accepts credit cards and is air-conditioned, and it is one of a cluster of
Kuala Lumpur eateries flagged in the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## Visiting note

A Bib Gourmand is a promise of value, not of fine-dining formality, so come for
the food rather than the setting. Because the Bib Gourmand list is refreshed each
year, it is worth checking the restaurant's current listing on the MICHELIN Guide
site before a special trip — and, with nasi ambeng built for sharing, bringing
company makes the most of the table.
