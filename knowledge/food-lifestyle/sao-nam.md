Behind a bright yellow façade on Tengkat Tong Shin, in the thick of Bukit
Bintang's eating streets, Sao Nam cooks Vietnamese food with enough colour and
character to earn a MICHELIN nod for value.

## What it is, and the Bib Gourmand

Sao Nam is a Vietnamese restaurant recognised in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur
& Penang with a **Bib Gourmand**, the guide's distinction for good quality
cooking at good value. The inspectors describe a room that "ticks all the boxes
for a fun-filled meal," with a colourful interior and a Vietnamese kitchen team
delivering specialities from across their home country — and note that some
dishes carry strong French influences, a reflection of Vietnam's own culinary
history. It sits in the $$ price band and is flagged as family-friendly.

## The dishes to order

The **phở bò** — beef noodle soup — is the iconic order, and the MICHELIN Guide
singles out its broth, which takes **over 10 hours** to make and "packs a tasty
punch." The other highlight is the **mangosteen-and-prawn salad**, praised for
big flavours and contrasting textures. Between them they capture Sao Nam's range:
a long-simmered classic on one hand, a bright tropical salad on the other.

## Where to find it

Sao Nam is at 25 Tengkat Tong Shin, Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur 50200, walking
distance from the district's shopping and nightlife. It is one of the Kuala
Lumpur entries in the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## Visiting note

The family-friendly tag and the cheerful room make Sao Nam an easy group booking
rather than a special-occasion splurge — in keeping with what a Bib Gourmand
signals. Because the award is reviewed annually, check the current MICHELIN
listing before a dedicated visit, and arrive ready for the phở bò, which is what
most people come for.
