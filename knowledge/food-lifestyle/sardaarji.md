Punjabi home cooking is not the first thing most visitors associate with George
Town's food streets, which is part of why Sardaarji stands out. The MICHELIN
Guide lists this Northern Indian restaurant with a
[Bib Gourmand](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia), its award for good food
at a fair price.

## What it is

Sardaarji serves authentic Northern Indian cooking with a Punjabi heart, from a
cheerful room on Lebuh China hung with colourful drapes and filled with Indian
music. It sits among the Bib Gourmand entries of the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur
& Penang — a value recommendation rather than a starred fine-dining room. The
name "Sardarji" is a respectful Punjabi honorific, chosen to greet every guest
with the same courtesy.

## What to order

MICHELIN points to the fish amritsari, a spicy red snapper curry, and the palak
paneer — a creamy spinach dish lifted with cumin — as the dishes to try, finishing
with the sweet, syrup-soaked gulab jamun. The cooking belongs to the Northern end
of the country's broad
[Indian-Malaysian table](/food-lifestyle/indian-malaysian-cuisine), distinct from
the South Indian banana-leaf style more common in Malaysia.

## Where to find it

The restaurant is at 6 Lebuh China, George Town, 10200, within walking distance
of the old quarter's heritage core. It opens daily for lunch and dinner.

## Background

Sardaarji began in 2018 as a family venture, started by a retiree and joined by
his relatives — a second-act project rather than a restaurant-group opening. That
home-kitchen origin shows in the cooking and is part of why MICHELIN inspectors
singled it out.

## Visiting note

The restaurant manages its own bookings, so reserve by contacting it directly. A
visit pairs naturally with a wider tour of [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).
