**Rasa Rasa** is the kind of place the MICHELIN Bib Gourmand was invented for:
a small, unshowy George Town room — raw-brick walls, a few industrial touches
softened by potted plants — serving honest [Nyonya
cooking](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine) at prices that barely climb
into double digits. MICHELIN lists it in the `$` band, its lowest, and awards it
the Bib for good value.

## A Bib Gourmand Peranakan room

The restaurant is one of the Penang names in the [MICHELIN Guide to Kuala Lumpur
and Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia), tagged "Family Friendly"
and run as a daytime operation rather than a dinner destination. The Bib
Gourmand it carries is not a star but a parallel distinction for affordable,
well-made food — which is exactly how the Guide frames Rasa Rasa.

## The food: a Nyonya set around nasi lemak

The menu is deliberately short. The dish MICHELIN builds its description around
is a Nyonya combo set centred on pandan nasi lemak with a choice of toppings —
a coconut-rice plate carrying the Peranakan kitchen's signatures. The Guide also
flags a five-spice chicken roll wrapped in tofu skin, and, for dessert, tub tim
krob, the crunchy water-chestnut-and-coconut-milk sweet. It is home-style Nyonya
food rather than a grand spread.

## Where it is

Rasa Rasa is at 59 Gat Lebuh Chulia, George Town 10300 — a quieter north-eastern
corner of the old town. MICHELIN lists it open from morning until about 16:00 on
trading days and closed on Wednesdays, with credit cards accepted and outdoor
seating that admits pets.

## Visiting note

Treat it as a breakfast or lunch stop, not a dinner plan: the kitchen shuts in
the afternoon. Come for the nasi lemak set, and leave room for the tub tim krob.
It sits naturally within a George Town [Penang-food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food)
crawl, offering the sit-down Nyonya counterpoint to the city's hawker stalls.
