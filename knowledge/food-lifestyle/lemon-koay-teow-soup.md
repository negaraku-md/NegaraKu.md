Across the channel from George Town, on the Penang mainland, Lemon Koay Teow Soup
has turned a simple bowl of noodles in pork broth into a MICHELIN-listed
breakfast — with one small twist that gives the stall its name.

## A Bib Gourmand mainland stall

Lemon Koay Teow Soup is listed in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang as a
**Bib Gourmand**, the guide's mark for good-quality cooking at a good value
rather than a star rating. It sits in the street-food category at the lowest ($)
price tier and is cash only. It is one of the Seberang Perai (mainland) entries
that broaden Penang's MICHELIN map beyond the island; for context, see
[the MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## The bowl

The dish is koay teow soup (*koay teow th'ng*): silky flat rice noodles in an
umami-laden pork-bone broth that the stall makes fresh daily. A **hand-chopped
pork patty**, which soaks up the broth, is a defining topping. The signature
touch is the **freshly squeezed lemon juice** set out on the table — a squeeze
lifts and brightens the savoury stock, a lighter alternative to the usual chilli
or vinegar. It is a comforting, clean-tasting bowl rather than a fiery one.

## Where it is

The MICHELIN listing gives the address as 4415 Lorong Cengal 5, Butterworth,
Seberang Perai, 13400, Penang. Butterworth is the mainland town opposite George
Town, linked by ferry and the Penang Bridge, and is part of the fast-growing
food scene on the [Penang mainland](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## Background

The MICHELIN description frames the stall as "the favourite breakfast of many
locals" — a neighbourhood fixture rather than a tourist stop. Its reputation
rests on the freshness of the daily broth and the quality of the pork patty, the
kind of single-dish focus typical of Penang's hawker culture.

## Visiting note

This is a morning stall and a cash-only one, so bring small notes and arrive
early. As with most popular Penang koay teow spots, the broth and noodles are
best in the first hours of trading, and busy mornings can mean a short wait.
