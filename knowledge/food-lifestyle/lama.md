Peranakan cooking is, at heart, home cooking — labour-intensive dishes passed
down through Straits-born Chinese families — and Lama leans into exactly that.
A father-and-daughter team runs the kitchen, and in 2026 the MICHELIN Guide
rewarded their home-style Nyonya food with a **Bib Gourmand**, naming Lama a new
entry in its Kuala Lumpur and Penang selection.

## A new Bib Gourmand for Nyonya cooking

Lama serves [Nyonya, or Peranakan, cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine)
— the fusion of Chinese ingredients and Malay spice pastes that grew up in the
old Straits ports. MICHELIN lists it in the mid-range ($$) tier and calls the
cooking authentic and home-style, most of it meant to be shared, which is how
Nyonya food is traditionally eaten: several dishes down the middle of the table
rather than one plate each. The Bib Gourmand recognises value rather than
luxury, and Lama's arrival in the 2026 list put it among the city's notable new
value entries. For the wider picture, see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## Signature dishes and the free-flow tea

A standout is the pie tee — crispy pastry cups (the "top hats" of Nyonya
cooking) filled with finely diced vegetables, fried onions and shrimp, dressed
in a house-made chilli sauce. It is a dish that rewards a kitchen willing to
fiddle, and it anchors a menu built for sharing. A small but telling touch:
MICHELIN notes the pandan lemongrass tea comes with unlimited free refills — the
kind of hospitality that signals a place cooking to feed people rather than to
impress them.

## A dining room of antiques

Part of Lama's appeal is the setting. The interior is exquisitely appointed and
doubles as a showcase for the owner's savvily curated antique collection, giving
the room the feel of a collector's home as much as a restaurant — an apt frame
for a cuisine so bound up with heritage and memory.

## Visiting

Lama is a sit-down restaurant rather than a hawker stall, so it suits a leisurely
shared meal. Confirm the current address before you go — the MICHELIN page places
it in Mont Kiara, while some other listings cite a different KL neighbourhood —
and, as with every Bib Gourmand, check the latest MICHELIN selection to confirm
the award still holds.
