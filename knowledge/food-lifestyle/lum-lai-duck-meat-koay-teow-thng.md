Among George Town's duck-noodle stalls, one has become a magnet for queues: a
market stand that has cooked the same bowl, the same way, for four decades, and
now carries a MICHELIN Bib Gourmand to prove the city agrees.

## A Bib Gourmand duck-noodle stall

Lum Lai Duck Meat Koay Teow Th'ng holds a MICHELIN **Bib Gourmand** — the mark
for good-quality cooking at good value — in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).
Its popularity has only grown since the recognition, and the inspectors warn to
expect queues at peak hours.

## The signature bowl

The dish is **koay teow th'ng**, a Teochew-origin bowl of flat rice noodles in a
clear broth — one of the classic styles of
[Malaysian noodles](/food-lifestyle/malaysian-noodles). Lum Lai's version is
built on duck, pork and fish cake. The broth is slow-simmered for at least two
hours with whole ducks, pork bones and offal until it is deeply umami; the duck
comes out silky, and each bowl is finished with white pepper, soy sauce,
scallions and golden fried garlic. A chilli-soy dip is served on the side.

## Where it is

The stall trades inside **Cecil Street Market (Pasar Lebuh Cecil), 24 Lebuh
Cecil, George Town, 10300** — a classic Penang market-hall setting among the
inner city's other [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) institutions. The
MICHELIN Guide lists it at the lowest ($) price level, and it is cash only.

## Background

The business was founded by **Lau Lum Lai**, who started out in the late 1970s
as a travelling pushcart hawker in George Town's working-class streets before
settling into the Cecil Street Market food court in 1991. It is now run by his
son, Lau Wei Loon, and daughter-in-law, Lim Sui Eng, who do the cooking
themselves and have kept the recipe steady for more than 40 years.

## Visiting

The stall opens early and sells through the day, but the best of the broth and
the queues both favour early arrivals. It manages its own custom at the counter
rather than taking bookings, and — as with every Bib Gourmand — the distinction
is reviewed each year, so check the current MICHELIN listing before you go.
