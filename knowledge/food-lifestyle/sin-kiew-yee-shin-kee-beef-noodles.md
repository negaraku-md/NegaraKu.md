Sin Kiew Yee Shin Kee Beef Noodles is a veteran noodle shop in the heart of
Kuala Lumpur's old city centre, and one of the beef-noodle specialists
recognised in the [MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
Kuala Lumpur & Penang selection.

## What it is

The shop carries a **Bib Gourmand**, the MICHELIN distinction for good food at
good value — the Guide's nod to a satisfying, well-priced meal rather than a
MICHELIN Star. It is filed in the lowest ($) price tier, and MICHELIN frames it
as a modest shop that has earned a loyal following. By the Guide's account,
"what started as a humble food stall back in 1949 is now a household name."

## The signature dishes

The draw is beef noodles, offered either in soup or "dressed in an aromatic
sauce" for a dry version. Diners choose the base — ribbon rice noodles, rice
vermicelli, or thin, bouncy **Hakka noodles**. The toppings are where the shop
makes its name: MICHELIN singles out the "signature springy beef meatballs and
sliced beef," and the thickly sliced beef tripe, "prized for its tender texture
and rich flavours." A home-made chilli sauce ties the bowl together.

## Location

Sin Kiew Yee Shin Kee is at **7a, Jalan Tun Tan Cheng Lock, Kuala Lumpur City
Centre**, a historic street in the old core of the capital near the Chinatown
district. It is a compact, informal shop; the MICHELIN Guide notes that
reservations are handled by the shop directly rather than through the Guide's
site.

## Beef noodles in context

Beef noodles are a staple of Malaysian Chinese cooking, and the Hakka-noodle
option here reflects the dish's roots in that community's kitchens. It sits
within the broader family of
[Malaysian noodle dishes](/food-lifestyle/malaysian-noodles), where regional and
dialect-group styles each shape the broth, the noodle and the garnish in their
own way.

## Visiting note

As a long-established, single-focus shop, Sin Kiew Yee Shin Kee rewards an early
visit — popular Bib Gourmand spots in the city centre can sell out or close for
the day once the lunch rush passes. Confirm opening hours before a special trip,
and treat the MICHELIN listing as the authoritative record of its current status.
