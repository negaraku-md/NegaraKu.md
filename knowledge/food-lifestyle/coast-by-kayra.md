Coast by Kayra is a Kerala-focused Indian restaurant inside The Starhill mall in
Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur, and one of the Indian eateries recognised in the
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur & Penang
selection.

## What it is

The restaurant carries a **Bib Gourmand**, the MICHELIN distinction for good food
at good value rather than a MICHELIN Star. It is filed in the $$ price tier, and
MICHELIN frames it as the sister establishment of Kayra that "stays true to its
sea-to-plate ethos" — a dining room dressed in turquoise accents that nod to the
ocean theme at the heart of the menu.

## The signature dishes

The kitchen reimagines traditional **Kerala** dishes in a more sophisticated
register, leaning on the state's coastal larder. Its signature is a **Kerala fish
curry**, which MICHELIN describes as "based on a family recipe, with raw mango and
yoghurt for acidity"; the Guide recommends ordering naan to soak up the sauce.
Seafood and produce are sourced locally — a deliberate choice, MICHELIN notes, to
support small farms and fishermen.

## Location

Coast by Kayra is at **Lot G03-G05, Ground Floor, The Starhill, 181 Jalan Bukit
Bintang, Kuala Lumpur 55100**, in the city's main shopping and dining district.
The MICHELIN Guide notes air conditioning, a car park, wheelchair access and that
the restaurant handles its own bookings.

## Kerala cooking in Kuala Lumpur

Coast's South Indian, seafood-forward menu broadens the city's Indian dining
beyond the familiar North Indian and Tamil staples. It sits within the wider
story of [Indian-Malaysian cuisine](/food-lifestyle/indian-malaysian-cuisine),
whose range runs from the coastal curries of Kerala to the Tamil
[banana-leaf rice](/food-lifestyle/banana-leaf-rice) meals found across the
country.

## Visiting note

As a sit-down restaurant in a central mall, Coast suits an unhurried lunch or
dinner, and the signature fish curry with naan is the natural starting point.
Confirm opening hours before a special trip, and treat the MICHELIN listing as
the authoritative record of its current status.
