**Ivy's Nyonya Cuisine** is a George Town fixture of the quiet, long-running
kind: a husband-and-wife restaurant where, by the MICHELIN Guide's account, Ivy
has cooked and her husband has waited tables for over 15 years. The Guide awards
it a Bib Gourmand — its mark for good cooking at good value — and lists it as
Peranakan in the `$$` band.

## A family-run Bib Gourmand

The restaurant is one of the Penang names in the [MICHELIN Guide to Kuala Lumpur
and Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia), tagged "Family Friendly"
and "Eat like a local." What the Guide keeps returning to is longevity and a
single pair of hands in the kitchen: even after a move to a larger room, it
notes, the menu stayed the same. The Bib Gourmand here recognises a dependable,
home-style [Nyonya](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine) kitchen rather
than a reinvention of it.

## The food: Kapitan chicken and Joo Hoo Char

MICHELIN steers diners to the sharing set menus, which gather the restaurant's
best-sellers. Two dishes it names are Kapitan chicken curry — a Peranakan curry
of Portuguese-Eurasian lineage, rich and aromatic — and Joo Hoo Char, a stir-fry
of finely shredded vegetables (and, traditionally, cuttlefish) in chilli paste,
eaten wrapped in lettuce. Together they sketch the two poles of a Nyonya table:
a deep braise and a bright, textured stir-fry.

## Where it is

The English MICHELIN listing gives 100-104 Jalan Kedah, George Town 10050, with
air conditioning, a car park and credit cards accepted. Reservations are
recommended. Note that some versions of the page still show an older Chow Thye
address, a trace of the restaurant's move — so confirm the current location when
you book.

## Visiting note

Book ahead, go as a group, and order the set menu to taste the range the kitchen
is known for. It makes a satisfying sit-down anchor to a [Penang
food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) itinerary otherwise built around stalls and
coffee shops.
