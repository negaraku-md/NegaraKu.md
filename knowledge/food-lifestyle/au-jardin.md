Au Jardin is a one-MICHELIN-star restaurant in George Town, Penang, serving
contemporary European cuisine inside a converted art space. It is run by
chef-proprietor Kim Hock Su.

## What it is

Au Jardin occupies a distressed, corrugated-metal building known as The
Warehouse within the Hin Bus Depot — a former bus depot turned art space — at
125 Jalan Timah in George Town. The MICHELIN Guide classifies it as European
Contemporary and lists it at a `$$$$` price level, noting a sleek, modern
interior behind the industrial shell. It is one of Penang's two
MICHELIN-starred restaurants and a fixture of
[George Town's food scene](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## MICHELIN recognition

Au Jardin was among the first restaurants to receive a MICHELIN star in the
inaugural [MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia),
announced in December 2022, and it has retained one star through the 2026
selection. Its chef de cuisine, Lim Yan You, was also recognised with the
MICHELIN Young Chef award in the 2023 edition. As MICHELIN reassesses its
selections each year, the distinction reflects the current edition.

## The food

The kitchen's defining feature is a **tasting menu that changes monthly**,
which — in the MICHELIN Guide's words — "showcases creative, sophisticated
European fare with subtle local twists." Two items recur in coverage of the
restaurant: a hay-aged Penang duck, made from local birds aged to concentrate
flavour and leaner texture, and homemade sourdough served with tomato butter.
The Guide marks the restaurant as a farm-to-table destination.

## The chef

Au Jardin is led by chef-proprietor **Kim Hock Su** (also written Su Kim Hock).
He is identified by the MICHELIN Guide as the chef-proprietor of this
MICHELIN-starred restaurant, though the listing gives little biographical
detail; the kitchen's monthly menus and local sourcing reflect his
European-technique, Penang-rooted approach.

## Visiting

The MICHELIN Guide lists Au Jardin as open Thursday to Sunday for lunch and
dinner and closed Monday to Wednesday, with counter dining, air conditioning and
card payment, and reservations available. Because the menu changes monthly and
hours differ between listing versions — and MICHELIN distinctions are reassessed
annually — it is worth confirming the current menu, service times and
reservation details with the restaurant before visiting.
