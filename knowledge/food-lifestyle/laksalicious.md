Few dishes say Penang like laksa, and on Jalan Hutton a small shop has turned its
devotion to the bowl into a MICHELIN **Bib Gourmand** — the guide's award for
good-quality, good-value cooking. At **Laksalicious**, the MICHELIN Guide says it
plainly: laksa is the main event.

## A cheerful little laksa shop

MICHELIN describes Laksalicious as a small place bubbling with character — a
caricature of the owner on the sign, a mural showing the signature dishes, and a
menu that knows what it is about. It is classed as **Malaysian** cuisine in the
guide's lowest ($) price band, which is where the Bib Gourmand so often rewards
honest, everyday cooking.

## Two laksa, one choice

The decision at the table is which laksa. The **Nyonya asam laksa** is a sour,
spicy fish broth — tamarind-bright and coconut-free. The **Nyonya laksa lemak**
is its opposite: a rich, creamy coconut-milk broth. Prawn crackers add crunch to
both. To understand how these two styles fit Malaysia's wider laksa family, see
our guide to [laksa](/food-lifestyle/laksa).

## Beyond the bowl

MICHELIN also flags the sides worth ordering: **crispy prawn fritters**, **nasi
ulam** (herb rice) and, to finish, **gula melaka cendol** sweetened with palm
sugar. Together they round the laksa out into a proper meal.

## Planning a visit

Laksalicious is at **117-117A Jalan Hutton, George Town, 10050**, an easy stop on
any George Town eating route. The shop is small, so timing matters; confirm
current hours and days with the restaurant before visiting. For more of the
island's specialities, see our guide to
[Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) and the overview of the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).
