Chim by Chef Noom is a Thai fine-dining restaurant in Kuala Lumpur holding one
MICHELIN star. It is the Malaysian outpost of Thai chef Thaninthorn "Noom"
Chantrawan.

## What it is

The restaurant occupies level two of TSLAW Tower (also known as Menara TS Law)
at 39 Jalan Kamuning, in the Imbi area of Kuala Lumpur. The MICHELIN Guide
classifies it as Thai contemporary and lists it at a `$$$$` price level. In the
Guide's description, it is "tucked inside an office building" and "serves Thai
dishes rooted in tradition but with creative twists and stylish plating." It is
one of a cluster of KL restaurants to hold MICHELIN stars in recent editions,
part of the city's growing
[fine-dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia) scene.

## MICHELIN recognition

Chim by Chef Noom was awarded one MICHELIN star in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
2025 edition — one of two new one-star restaurants named that year — and
retained the star in the 2026 selection announced on 11 November 2025. The
front-of-house has also been recognised: the restaurant's Azmi Ahmad Kamal
received the MICHELIN Guide Service Award in 2025. MICHELIN reassesses its
selections annually, so the distinction reflects the current edition.

## The food

The kitchen serves elevated, contemporary Thai cuisine built on seasonal
multi-course tasting menus that change through the year, pairing Thai
technique and local produce with Japanese ingredients. The most frequently
cited signature is **"The Lost Recipe"**, which the MICHELIN Guide describes as
a 200-year-old variation of *tom yum* with carefully balanced flavours.

## The chef

Chim by Chef Noom is the Malaysian venture of **Thaninthorn "Noom"
Chantrawan**, a Thai chef who also runs the one-MICHELIN-star Chim by Siam
Wisdom in Bangkok. The Kuala Lumpur restaurant opened in 2024 and earned its
MICHELIN star within its first year of operation, making it one of a small
number of Klang Valley restaurants to do so.

## Visiting

The MICHELIN Guide lists Chim by Chef Noom as open for dinner Tuesday to Sunday
and closed on Monday, with parking, air conditioning and card payment, and
bookings handled by the restaurant directly. As tasting menus, service times and
MICHELIN distinctions all change over time, confirm the current menu format,
hours and reservation details with the restaurant before planning a visit.
