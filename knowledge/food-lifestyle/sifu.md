Peranakan cooking is Penang's heart cuisine, and on Jalan Pintal Tali one small
kitchen makes the case for it with a MICHELIN **Bib Gourmand** — the guide's
award for good-quality, good-value cooking. **Sifu** serves the Nyonya classics
the way a seasoned home cook would, and the guide's distinction says they are
worth the trip.

## A Bib Gourmand for Nyonya classics

MICHELIN classes Sifu as **Peranakan** and prices it in the $$ band — the
mid-range register where the Bib Gourmand so often lands. The menu runs to about
**40 à la carte dishes**, covering the staples of traditional Peranakan cooking
rather than chasing novelty. For the wider story of this cuisine, see our guide
to [Nyonya and Peranakan cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine).

## What to order

Two dishes anchor the table. The signature **asam prawn** arrives springy in a
dark brown, tamarind-heavy sauce balanced between sweet and sour. The **mang kuang
char** — sautéed shredded yam bean with dried squid and mushrooms — is prized for
its sweetness and crisp texture. Both are the kind of unshowy, deeply seasoned
plates that define good Nyonya home cooking.

## The cook behind it

MICHELIN singles out the person at the stove: the kitchen is **helmed by a lady
in her 70s who has been cooking for decades**. That continuity — one experienced
hand, a fixed repertoire of classics — is much of why the food tastes the way it
does.

## Planning a visit

Sifu is at **154 Jalan Pintal Tali, George Town, 10100**. MICHELIN notes that
reservations are not available through its site, so contact the restaurant
directly to ask about booking and current hours. For the broader context, see our
overview of the [MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).
