In a city where asam laksa is close to a civic obsession, a Bib Gourmand for it
means something. My Own Cafe, a small family shop on Lebuh Cannon, carries that
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) distinction for its
laksa — awarded for good cooking at a gentle price.

## What it is

My Own Cafe is a charming, family-run street-food shop in George Town, Penang,
listed among the Bib Gourmand entries of the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur &
Penang. It is a value recommendation, not a starred room — the kind of modest
place MICHELIN's Bib Gourmand exists to flag.

## The signature bowls

The menu is built around two laksas. The Penang asam laksa is the star: MICHELIN
describes its light, refreshing red soup as striking the right balance of sour
and spicy, topped off with mint and pineapple. Alongside it the kitchen serves a
richer Nyonya laksa — drawn from
[Peranakan cooking](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine) — and
vegetable-stuffed fried spring rolls that MICHELIN rates just as highly.

## Where to find it

The shop is at 2 Lebuh Cannon, George Town, 10200, steps from the historic Khoo
Kongsi clan house, placing it squarely on the heritage-core
[Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) trail.

## A family kitchen

The appeal is its smallness: a family running a short, focused menu from a shop
in the old quarter, rather than a large operation. That is the texture MICHELIN's
Bib Gourmand is meant to reward.

## Visiting note

It is cash-only and handles its own bookings, so carry cash and confirm the day's
opening hours before you arrive.
