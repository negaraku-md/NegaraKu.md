Thai restaurants are not what first comes to mind in George Town, a city
synonymous with its own hawker canon. Yet one of the quarter's
MICHELIN-recognised addresses is a Thai kitchen, and it earns the nod on the
strength of fresh seafood and a confident hand with the classics.

## A Bib Gourmand Thai kitchen

Thara holds a MICHELIN **Bib Gourmand** — the guide's mark for good-quality
cooking at good value — in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).
It is a distinction separate from the MICHELIN stars: where the stars reward a
particular level of cuisine, the Bib Gourmand rewards a satisfying meal at a
gentle price, and the inspectors note Thara's generous portions and friendly
prices.

## What to eat

The kitchen's focus is authentic Thai cooking built around fresh seafood. The
MICHELIN inspectors single out the restaurant's array of **tom yum** soup
variations, each made with different seafood, and name the **miang kam**
appetiser — fresh betel leaves wrapped around aromatics and dressed with a sweet
miang kam sauce — as a must-try first course.

## Where it is

Thara occupies a lofty room decorated in soothing green tones at **5 Lorong
Prangin, George Town, 10100**, within easy reach of the inner city's other
[Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) landmarks. The listing places it at a
moderate ($$) price level, higher than the city's hawker stalls but modest for a
sit-down restaurant.

## Background

The MICHELIN entry credits the chef with more than 15 years of culinary
experience, cooking authentic dishes from his home country. Beyond that the
public record is thin on the restaurant's founding and ownership, so the fuller
story of how Thara came to George Town is best confirmed with the restaurant
directly.

## Visiting

Thara manages its own bookings, so contact the restaurant directly rather than
reserving through the guide. Hours and opening days shift from time to time —
and, like every Bib Gourmand, the distinction is reviewed each year — so check
the current MICHELIN listing before you make the trip.
