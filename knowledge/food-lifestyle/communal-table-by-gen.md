**Communal Table by Gēn** is the most contemporary of George Town's Bib
Gourmand names: a counter-led room on Lebuh Presgrave where an open kitchen
turns out a short, modern menu that folds [Peranakan and
Malaysian](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine) cooking into something of
its own. MICHELIN lists it as Malaysian in the `$$` band and awards it a Bib
Gourmand for good cooking at good value.

## A modern Bib Gourmand

The restaurant is among the Penang entries in the [MICHELIN Guide to Kuala
Lumpur and Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia). The Guide
describes a casual, relaxing space built around an open kitchen, a long counter
table and floor-to-ceiling windows — a format that puts the cooking, and the
diners, on show. MICHELIN also notes that the restaurant's chef received the
Malaysian MICHELIN Guide Young Chef Award 2024, marking it as a kitchen on the
rise rather than an old-guard institution.

## The food: duck bun and local gelato

Dinner is a small à la carte selection of modern dishes made with local
ingredients, melding Peranakan and Malaysian flavours. The dish MICHELIN
singles out is an aged duck breast served in a steamed bun — crispy skin, a
"secret sauce" — a neat emblem of the kitchen's cross-cultural bent. To finish,
the Guide points to homemade gelato in distinctly local flavours, including
nutmeg harumanis and hor ka sai.

## Where it is

Communal Table by Gēn is at 68 Lebuh Presgrave, George Town 10300. MICHELIN
lists it as a mostly dinner operation (roughly 17:00-23:00), closed on
Wednesdays, with weekend lunch and dinner service, counter seating, air
conditioning and an interesting wine list. Hours vary between the Guide's
locale pages, so confirm before visiting.

## Visiting note

Book ahead and take a counter seat if you can — the open kitchen is part of the
experience. It is the modern bookend to a George Town [food
tour](/food-lifestyle/penang-food), showing how the city's Peranakan and
Malaysian traditions are being reworked by a younger kitchen.
