Tucked into Taman Kok Lian, north of central Kuala Lumpur, a stall named for
Jalan Ipoh has been turning out one dish, cooked one slow way, for decades — and
it has the MICHELIN recognition to show for it.

## What it is, and the Bib Gourmand

Jalan Ipoh Claypot Chicken Rice is a street-food stall recognised in the MICHELIN
Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang with a **Bib Gourmand**, the guide's mark of good
quality at good value. It was among the Bib Gourmand picks in the edition
announced in November 2023. The listing sits in the lowest **$** (budget) price
band — proof that MICHELIN recognition in Malaysia reaches humble hawker stalls,
not just restaurants.

## The dish

For more than 30 years the stall has cooked **claypot chicken rice to order over
charcoal**. The method is the point: each individual claypot is fired slowly, so
the rice at the bottom forms a prized **crispy crust**. The MICHELIN Guide notes
it comes with **Cantonese pork sausage** (lap cheong) and ginger, and that you
can add **salted fish**, which imparts extra aroma. It is one of Malaysia's
enduring [rice dishes](/food-lifestyle/malaysian-rice-dishes) done with real
patience.

## Where to find it

The stall is at Lot 1224, 7 Jalan Batu Ambar, Taman Kok Lian, Kuala Lumpur 51200,
open roughly 10am to 5pm and closed on Wednesdays. It is **cash-only** and
**walk-in only** — no card payments and no reservations — and features in the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## Visiting note

Charcoal claypots are cooked one at a time, so patience is part of the deal;
arrive expecting a wait at peak hours and carry cash. Hawker-stall hours and
closed days shift more often than a restaurant's, and the Bib Gourmand list is
reviewed each year, so a quick check before you set out is worthwhile.
