Most of Penang's MICHELIN fame clusters on George Town's island streets. Taman
Bukit Curry Mee is a reminder that the mainland keeps its own treasures — and that
a morning bowl of curry mee in Bukit Mertajam is worth crossing the channel for.

## A Bib Gourmand curry mee

Taman Bukit Curry Mee is listed as a **MICHELIN Bib Gourmand** in the MICHELIN
Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang selection, the award for good food at good value. It
deals in a single Penang classic: [curry mee](/food-lifestyle/curry-mee), the
coconut-and-curry noodle soup that is the island's answer to the richer,
southern-style laksa lemak.

## The signature bowl

The draw is a broth MICHELIN describes as creamy with coconut milk and built on a
spicy curry paste, poured over noodles and crowned with **half-cooked cockles**
that the guide calls briny-sweet and bursting with juices. It is a comforting,
assertive bowl — the kind of everyday hawker cooking the Bib Gourmand was created
to honour, and a staple of wider [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## Location and background

The stall is on **Jalan Bukit Kecil, Taman Bukit, Bukit Mertajam**, in Seberang
Perai (14000) — mainland Penang rather than George Town island. It trades from a
tucked-away food court and has sold curry mee here for decades, long enough to
become a fixture that locals direct visitors toward.

## Visiting

This is an early riser's stop: MICHELIN lists morning hours of roughly 06:00 to
09:00, Tuesday to Sunday and closed Mondays, and the noodles frequently sell out
before the morning is over. Reports of the exact hours vary, so plan to arrive
early and confirm the day before you set out from the island.
