Molina is an innovative fine-dining restaurant perched on the 51st floor of THE
FACE Style in Kuala Lumpur. It holds One MICHELIN Star in the
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur & Penang,
where its listing pairs inventive, border-crossing cooking with panoramic views
of the city skyline.

## A one-MICHELIN-star restaurant

Molina holds **One MICHELIN Star**, the guide's distinction for "high-quality
cooking" that is "worth a stop," and appears in MICHELIN's current full
selection for Malaysia. Secondary reporting notes that the restaurant earned its
star within months of opening — an unusually fast recognition — and separately
received a MICHELIN Opening of the Year award. The star places it among Kuala
Lumpur's leading [fine-dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia) rooms.

## The food: innovative and border-crossing

The MICHELIN Guide classifies Molina's cuisine as **Innovative**. Its listing
describes inventive dishes that blend French, Nordic and Asian influences, with
seafood and vegetables as particular strengths; examples cited by the guide
include Dutch mussels with pickled banana and cauliflower, and a chanterelle and
rum-raisin dessert. Secondary coverage links the restaurant's modern European
direction to Dutch chef Sidney Schutte, who holds two MICHELIN stars at Spectrum
in Amsterdam, with Guillaume Depoortere named as head chef; sources differ on
the precise division of roles.

## Setting: a 51st-floor rooftop

Molina occupies the 51st floor of THE FACE Style, 1020 Jalan Sultan Ismail,
Kampung Baru. The MICHELIN Guide notes that, "perched atop a skyscraper, the
restaurant commands panoramic vistas of KL's skyline," and lists facilities
including a "great view," an interesting wine list and valet parking. The guide
flags it as good for a date night.

## Visiting Molina

Per its MICHELIN listing, Molina serves dinner Tuesday to Saturday, with a
Saturday lunch service, and is closed on Mondays and Sundays. The guide places
it in the top (`$$$$`) price band, in line with its tasting-menu format, which
secondary coverage describes as a lengthy experience. The restaurant handles its
own reservations, so booking ahead by contacting it directly is advisable. As
with every MICHELIN entry, the star reflects the guide's most recent published
selection and is reviewed each year.
