A corner stall in Pudu opens for only a few hours at lunch, and the queue starts
well before it does. People are there for one thing: Wong Mei Kee's siew yok,
the roast pork that earned the stall a MICHELIN Guide Bib Gourmand.

## One dish, done exceptionally

Wong Mei Kee is classified by the MICHELIN Guide as **Street Food** and
recognised with a **Bib Gourmand** — its mark of good quality cooking at good
value. The draw is **siew yok**, Cantonese roast pork with crackling skin and
succulent meat, a cornerstone of
[Chinese-Malaysian cooking](/food-lifestyle/chinese-malaysian-cuisine). "Open
for just a few hours a day," the guide writes, "this stall attracts foodies in
droves with its coveted siew yok." It was recognised in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang 2023 selection, and sits in the $ (least expensive) band.

## Worth queueing for

MICHELIN tags the stall "Worth Queueing For" and gives plain advice: "Arrive
early and wait for the pork to come out of the oven at noon sharp." Because it
trades for only a short window each day, the roast pork is batch-cooked and can
sell out — the queue is part of the experience, not a sign to come back later.

## Visiting note

Wong Mei Kee is at 30 Jalan Nyonya, off Jalan Pudu, Kuala Lumpur. It opens only
at lunchtime on selected days and manages its own bookings. It is one of the
street-food entries on the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Bib Gourmand
list, which is refreshed each year — so confirm the current hours and status
before making a special trip.
