Penang takes its curry noodles seriously, and Hot Bowl White Curry Mee has been
feeding that devotion from a modest shopfront on Jalan Rangoon since 1992. The
MICHELIN Guide lists it with a [Bib Gourmand](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia),
the award reserved for good cooking at a friendly price.

## What it is

Hot Bowl White Curry Mee is a longstanding, family-run noodle shop in George
Town, Penang. It sits among the Bib Gourmand entries of the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang — not a starred restaurant, but a value-focused recommendation
for a dish done well. The owner-chef still works hands-on in the kitchen.

## The signature bowl

The draw is the white curry mee. Rather than drowning the noodles in a fiery
red gravy, the kitchen serves them in a pale, coconut milk-based broth and sets
the chilli down beside you: a bowl of curry paste made with spices, dried shrimps
and chillies that, MICHELIN notes, is hand-stirred for roughly two hours. You
stir in as much as you want, which is why "white" curry mee reads mild until you
make it otherwise — the balance between spicy and sweet is yours to set. It is a
gentler cousin of Penang's better-known red [curry mee](/food-lifestyle/curry-mee).

## Where to find it

The shop is at 58C Jalan Rangoon, George Town, 10400, in the thick of the city's
food-dense streets. It is cash-only, keeps limited hours and does not take online
reservations, so it rewards an early, planned visit over a late walk-in.

## A Penang institution

Opening in 1992, Hot Bowl earned its following the slow way — a single dish,
made consistently, for more than three decades. That longevity is part of why it
reads as a genuine piece of George Town's hawker fabric rather than a trend, and
why it belongs on any map of [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## Visiting note

Because the hours are tight and the shop is cash-only, check the day's opening
times before you set out, and come with an appetite for one thing done properly.
