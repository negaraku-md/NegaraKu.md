MICHELIN recognition in Penang tends to cluster in George Town, but one of the
state's most single-minded Bib Gourmand holders sits across the water on the
mainland — a cart that has sold exactly one thing, the same way, since 1962.

## A one-dish Bib Gourmand cart

BM Cathay Pancake holds a MICHELIN **Bib Gourmand** — the mark for good-quality
cooking at good value — in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia),
where the inspectors tag it as Worth Queueing For. The recognition is unusual
for a stall with a menu of one.

## The one dish

The cart serves only **apam balik**, a Malaysian griddle pancake folded over a
sweet filling. BM Cathay's version is chargrilled and packed with sugar and
peanuts, cooked to order so each one arrives hot — crisp on the outside, chewy
within. It is a fixture of Penang's wider
[food culture](/food-lifestyle/penang-food), elevated here by decades of
repetition of a single recipe.

## Where it is

The cart stands **next to the entrance of the Cathay Food Court on Jalan Aston,
Bukit Mertajam, Seberang Perai, 14000** — on the mainland (Seberang Perai) side
of Penang rather than on the island. It is listed at the lowest ($) price level
and is cash only.

## Background

The MICHELIN entry dates the cart to **1962**, since when it has sold nothing but
apam balik. That focus — one pancake, made the same way for more than sixty
years — is much of what the Bib Gourmand is rewarding.

## Visiting

Expect long queues at peak hours, and note that the cart closes on some weekdays,
so plan around its trading days. It takes no bookings, and — as with every Bib
Gourmand — the distinction is reviewed each year, so check the current MICHELIN
listing before you go.
