The name promises duck blood — a traditional curry-mee topping — but what the
MICHELIN Guide praises most about this Jalan Burma shop is its broth, its roast
pork and its chilli sauce.

## What it is

Duck Blood Curry Mee is a street-food shop recognised in the MICHELIN Guide as a
**Bib Gourmand** pick — the Guide's distinction for good-quality, good-value
cooking, often given to hawker and café-style spots (see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) for how the
distinctions work). The listing describes it as hidden inside a Malaysian-style
café; it is air-conditioned, cash-only, and not available for reservations.

## The signature bowl

The shop serves [curry mee](/food-lifestyle/curry-mee) — noodles in a
coconut-milk curry broth. The MICHELIN listing describes a "savoury-sweet white
curry base" made with coconut milk that is "freshly prepared every day." Of the
toppings on offer, the Guide singles out the roasted pork as "phenomenal," and
urges diners to try the homemade chilli sauce. The duck blood of the name is a
classic curry-mee addition, though the Guide's own write-up concentrates on the
broth, the pork and the chilli.

## Where to find it

The shop is at 223B Jalan Burma, George Town, 10050, Penang. The MICHELIN listing
shows it open from 12:00 to 22:00 on most days and closed on Thursday — longer,
later hours than many hawker stalls, though a shop's schedule can still change.

## Before you go

Bring cash, and note that the café setting is air-conditioned — a comfortable
option in George Town's heat. There are no bookings. Because the MICHELIN
selection is reviewed each year, check the current Guide if the distinction is
what brings you.
