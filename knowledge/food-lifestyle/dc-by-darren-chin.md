DC. by Darren Chin is a French contemporary fine-dining restaurant in Taman Tun
Dr Ismail (TTDI), on the western side of Kuala Lumpur, from chef-owner Darren
Chin. It holds One MICHELIN Star in the
[MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur & Penang,
where its listing describes French cooking given a personal, globally inflected
turn.

## A one-MICHELIN-star restaurant

DC. by Darren Chin holds **One MICHELIN Star**, the guide's distinction for
"high-quality cooking" that is "worth a stop," and appears in MICHELIN's current
full selection for Malaysia. Secondary reporting records that it was among the
restaurants awarded a star in Malaysia's inaugural MICHELIN guide and has
retained the star through subsequent editions. It is one of the longest-standing
entries in the country's [fine-dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia)
selection.

## The food: a take on French traditions

The MICHELIN Guide classifies the cuisine as **French Contemporary**. Its
listing states that "the kitchen delivers a unique take on French traditions,"
carrying global influences and Malaysian accents, and advises guests to "check
out the French cheese trolley" and the wine list. Secondary coverage notes that
chef-owner Darren Chin trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris and that the
restaurant is known for a restrained, produce-led style; those details should be
confirmed against primary interviews.

## Setting: a three-storey restaurant

Per the MICHELIN Guide, the restaurant spans three storeys and "in addition to
bars and dining rooms, also sports a Louis XIII-themed private room." The listing
gives the address as 44 Persiaran Zaaba, Taman Tun Dr Ismail, Kuala Lumpur, and
lists facilities including air conditioning and an interesting wine list. (Older
listings record a different address, so the current location is worth confirming
before a visit.)

## Visiting DC. by Darren Chin

Per its MICHELIN listing, the restaurant serves dinner Wednesday to Sunday with
a Friday and Saturday lunch service, and is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. The
guide places it in the top (`$$$$`) price band. The restaurant manages its own
reservations, so booking ahead by contacting it directly is advisable. As with
every MICHELIN entry, the star reflects the guide's most recent published
selection and is reviewed each year.
