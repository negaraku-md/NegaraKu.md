Roti by d'Tandoor is a North Indian restaurant tucked into a residential house
off Jalan Ampang in Kuala Lumpur, and one of the Indian eateries recognised in
the [MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Kuala Lumpur &
Penang selection.

## What it is

The restaurant carries a **Bib Gourmand**, the MICHELIN distinction for good food
at good value rather than a MICHELIN Star. Filed in the $$ price tier, it is
described by the Guide as offering "a heartwarming range of fine Indian cuisine"
built on family recipes and a careful blend of herbs and spices. The setting — a
house in a quiet residential district, with outdoor seating and a family-friendly
feel — is part of the appeal.

## The signature dishes

The kitchen leans North Indian. MICHELIN's inspectors single out the **butter
chicken masala** and the naan — soft and chewy, and ideal for soaking up the rich
gravies — while **kulfi**, the dense Indian ice cream, comes in several flavours
to finish. A few dishes require pre-ordering, so it pays to ask when you book.

## Location

Roti by d'Tandoor is at **82 Jalan Damai, off Jalan Ampang, Kampung Datuk
Keramat, Kuala Lumpur 55000**, in an established neighbourhood near the embassy
belt east of the city centre. The MICHELIN Guide notes air conditioning, a car
park and a terrace, and that the restaurant manages its own bookings.

## The d'Tandoor name

Roti is part of the broader **d'Tandoor** brand, which the MICHELIN Guide says
"opened in Malaysia in 1990" and has since spread to other countries. That
lineage places it within the long story of
[Indian-Malaysian cuisine](/food-lifestyle/indian-malaysian-cuisine) — a
tradition that runs from the North Indian tandoor kitchens to the Tamil
[banana-leaf rice](/food-lifestyle/banana-leaf-rice) shops that define much of
Malaysia's Indian dining.

## Visiting note

As a sit-down restaurant rather than a quick stop, Roti by d'Tandoor suits a
relaxed lunch or dinner, and pre-ordering the dishes that need it will get the
most from a visit. Confirm opening hours and any pre-order items when booking,
and treat the MICHELIN listing as the authoritative record of its current status.
