Some of Penang's best food comes from a cart, not a kitchen, and Super Star Koay
Teow Soup is proof. Trading on Lebuh Kimberley since 1960, it carries a MICHELIN
Guide [Bib Gourmand](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) and a standing
queue to match.

## What it is

Super Star is a street-food cart in George Town, Penang, listed among the Bib
Gourmand entries of the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang — a value pick
rather than a starred restaurant. MICHELIN also tags it "Worth Queueing For," so
a wait is part of the experience.

## The signature bowl

The must-try is koay teow soup: flat rice noodles in a clear broth with chicken,
pork, fish balls and pork liver. The sleeper hit is the side — chicken feet
braised in a spiced soy marinade, which MICHELIN calls "simply divine." Together
they are a compact lesson in Penang's Teochew-rooted hawker cooking.

## Where to find it

The cart is at 78 Lebuh Kimberley, George Town, 10100, on one of the old
quarter's densest [food streets](/food-lifestyle/penang-food). It is cash-only
and closed on Wednesdays.

## Three generations on

A single cart run by one family since 1960, now in its third generation, is
exactly the kind of long-lived hawker operation MICHELIN's Bib Gourmand was
created to recognise — consistency over decades, at hawker prices.

## Visiting note

Expect a queue at peak times, bring cash, avoid Wednesdays, and confirm the
address and hours before you set out.
