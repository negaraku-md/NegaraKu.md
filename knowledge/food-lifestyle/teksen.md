Tucked into a shophouse on Lebuh Carnarvon, Teksen is one of George Town's most
reliable tables — a plain, busy dining room where the cooking, not the decor,
does the talking. The MICHELIN Guide awards it a **Bib Gourmand**, its mark for
good-quality, good-value cooking, and tags the place as "worth queueing for."

## A George Town institution

Teksen is a casual Cantonese restaurant that has been feeding Penang since
**1965**. The room is clean and simple, with a few festive touches, and it draws
a steady crowd of food lovers rather than a scene. The Bib Gourmand sits just
below MICHELIN's star tier: it rewards kitchens that cook well and charge fairly,
which is exactly Teksen's register — a mid-range (MICHELIN $$) bill for
confident, unfussy food.

## What to order

The house signature is the **deep-fried house-made tofu with dried local
anchovies** — lightly seasoned, golden and very savoury. Beyond it, the menu runs
from well-made traditional Cantonese dishes to fusion plates given a local twist,
the kind of cooking that rewards ordering a spread to share. The MICHELIN Guide
also suggests asking about the **daily double-boiled soup**, which changes with
what the kitchen has on.

## Where it is

Teksen is at **18 & 20 Lebuh Carnarvon, George Town, 10100**, in the heart of the
UNESCO-listed old town and within walking distance of much of Penang's street-food
circuit. It is an easy stop to fold into a day of eating across George Town.

## Planning a visit

Come hungry and come early. MICHELIN warns of long queues at peak hours, and
reported opening days differ between listings, so it is worth confirming hours
with the restaurant before you make the trip. For the wider context of Penang's
MICHELIN recognition, see our overview of the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) and the
guide to [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).
