Akar is a modern Malaysian fine-dining restaurant in Taman Tun Dr Ismail
(TTDI), a residential neighbourhood on the western side of Kuala Lumpur. It
holds One MICHELIN Star in the [MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
Kuala Lumpur & Penang, where its listing describes a set menu that filters
European technique through Japanese, French and Malaysian influences.

## A one-MICHELIN-star restaurant

Akar holds **One MICHELIN Star**, the guide's distinction for "high-quality
cooking" that is "worth a stop." In MICHELIN's current full selection for
Malaysia, Akar is listed among the one-star restaurants and is flagged as a
newly promoted entry. A single star still places it in a small group of
Malaysian restaurants recognised by the guide, part of the wider map of the
country's [fine dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia).

## The food: European recipes, Malaysian roots

The MICHELIN Guide classifies Akar's cuisine as **Malaysian and Contemporary**.
Its description notes that the restaurant is shaped by the chef's time in Japan
and France and draws on his Malaysian roots: in MICHELIN's words, the chef
"refashions European recipes with Japanese, French and Malaysian influences."
The menu is a seasonal set menu the guide calls "a culinary journey inspired by
his personal history and memories," using local ingredients and techniques that
include cooking in melted beeswax, dry-ageing and searing in claypot. Local rice
wine and sake pairings are offered. This blend of imported technique and
Malaysian produce sits alongside the country's broader
[culinary traditions](/food-lifestyle/national-dishes-of-malaysia).

## Setting and location

Akar is at 109 Jalan Aminuddin Baki, Taman Tun Dr Ismail, Kuala Lumpur. The
dining room is described as an intimate, cosy, air-conditioned space with an
open kitchen. The MICHELIN Guide flags the restaurant as good for solo dining.

## Visiting Akar

Per its MICHELIN listing, Akar serves dinner from Tuesday to Saturday with lunch
on Friday and Saturday, and is closed on Mondays and Sundays. The guide places
it in the `$$$` price band. Because it is a small fine-dining room serving a set
menu, booking ahead is advisable. As with every MICHELIN entry, the star
reflects the guide's most recent published selection and is reviewed each year.
