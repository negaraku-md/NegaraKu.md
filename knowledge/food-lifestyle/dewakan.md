Dewakan sits on the 48th floor of Naza Tower in Kuala Lumpur's Platinum Park,
a fine-dining restaurant that the [MICHELIN Guide](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
has placed at the very top of Malaysia's dining scene. Its name is glossed by
the guide as meaning "food from God," and its kitchen, led by chef Darren Teoh,
builds an entirely Malaysian menu from local and endemic ingredients.

## A two-MICHELIN-star restaurant

Dewakan holds **Two MICHELIN Stars** in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur &
Penang — a distinction MICHELIN reserves for cooking that is "excellent" and
"worth a detour." According to MICHELIN's own full-selection listing, it is the
only two-star restaurant in Malaysia, the highest rating in the country's
selection. Secondary reporting records that Dewakan was first awarded one star
in the inaugural Malaysian guide, was promoted to two stars the following
edition, and has retained them since.

Alongside its stars, Dewakan carries a **MICHELIN Green Star**, the guide's
distinction for sustainability in gastronomy; press coverage describes it as the
first Green Star given to a Malaysian restaurant. A two-star rating is rare
anywhere in the world, which places Dewakan among Malaysia's most significant
entries in international [fine dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia).

## The food: indigenous and endemic ingredients

The MICHELIN Guide classifies Dewakan's cuisine as **Malaysian and
Contemporary**. The restaurant serves a tasting menu that MICHELIN describes as
"Malaysian down to its very core," built around locally sourced produce, with
"many items that are fermented or dry-aged in-house." In its sustainability
statement to the guide, the kitchen describes a nose-to-tail approach centred on
local, native and endemic ingredients, with fermenting and preserving used to
extend seasonal windows. MICHELIN notes that even the dinnerware is handmade by
local artists.

This focus on the Malaysian larder sets Dewakan apart from restaurants working
in imported European idioms, and connects it to the wider story of the
country's [national dishes and ingredients](/food-lifestyle/national-dishes-of-malaysia).

## Setting: the 48th floor of Naza Tower

The restaurant occupies the 48th floor of Naza Tower (Skyviews), at Platinum
Park, 10 Persiaran KLCC. The MICHELIN Guide highlights the "jaw-dropping city
views" from that height. The dining room is a formal, air-conditioned
fine-dining space; the guide lists car parking and notes that the venue suits a
special occasion.

## Chef Darren Teoh

Dewakan's kitchen is led by chef Darren Teoh, named in MICHELIN's listing as the
restaurant's chef. Teoh has become one of the most prominent figures in
Malaysian fine dining through Dewakan's rise to two stars. Beyond the detail
recorded by the guide and reputable press, further biographical specifics should
be confirmed against primary interviews.

## Visiting Dewakan

Dewakan is a dinner-focused fine-dining restaurant, open in the evenings and
closed on Sundays per its MICHELIN listing. The guide places it in the top
(`$$$$`) price band, so diners should expect tasting-menu pricing. The
restaurant manages its own reservations rather than taking them through the
MICHELIN platform, so booking ahead by contacting the restaurant directly is
essential. As with every MICHELIN entry, the award reflects the guide's most
recent published selection and is reviewed each year.
