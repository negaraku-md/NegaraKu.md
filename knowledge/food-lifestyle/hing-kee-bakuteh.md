Order bak kut teh in Kuala Lumpur and you are tapping into what many Malaysians
treat as soul food — a herbal pork-rib soup simmered until the aromatics and the
meat fuse. Hing Kee Bakuteh, on Jalan Kepong, is one of the city's reliably good
versions, and the MICHELIN Guide agrees: it carries a **Bib Gourmand**, the
inspectors' mark for good quality at a kind price.

## A Bib Gourmand bak kut teh house

[Bak kut teh](/food-lifestyle/bak-kut-teh) is often described as an iconic
Malaysian dish, and Hing Kee has built its reputation on doing it well rather
than doing it fancy. The MICHELIN Guide lists it under Malaysian cuisine in the
budget ($) price tier and awards it a Bib Gourmand — a distinction that sits
apart from the Guide's stars and specifically flags value for money. For the
wider picture of how the Guide operates in Malaysia, see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## The soup — and a drier, richer version

The house style, by MICHELIN's own account, leans gentle: the soup bak kut teh
has a characteristically mild flavour, the kind you can drink bowl after bowl
without fatigue. For those who want something with more heft, there is a dry
version — simmered down to a richer, thicker, spicier consistency that clings to
the ribs rather than swimming around them. Between the two, the kitchen covers
both ends of the bak kut teh spectrum: the clear, restorative broth and the
dark, concentrated plate.

## Finding it on Jalan Kepong

Hing Kee sits at 121 Jalan Kepong, in Taman Usahawan. It is not a cramped stall
but a sprawl: the operation runs across four shophouses lined up along the
boulevard, which gives it plenty of seating — useful, because MICHELIN notes the
place is particularly popular at lunch, when nearby workers and bak kut teh
regulars crowd in.

## Visiting

Hing Kee is an everyday, walk-in sort of place rather than a reservations
restaurant, so expect to queue at peak times. Hours skew late and vary by day,
and the kitchen is frequently closed on Mondays, so it is worth checking the
current schedule before making a special trip. As with every Bib Gourmand, the
award is reviewed each year, so a quick look at the latest MICHELIN selection is
the surest way to confirm it still holds.
