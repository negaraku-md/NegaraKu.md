Among the newest names on Penang's MICHELIN map is a George Town coffeeshop that
has been feeding the neighbourhood since the early 1990s — proof that a humble
halal hawker kitchen can stand beside the island's most celebrated stalls.

## A Bib Gourmand coffeeshop

Bee Hwa Cafe is listed as a **MICHELIN Bib Gourmand** in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang selection, where it was added as a new Penang entry. The Bib
Gourmand marks places serving good food at good value — a tier below the MICHELIN
Star but a sought-after endorsement. What sets Bee Hwa apart on the list is that
it is a **halal** kitchen: MICHELIN notes it uses chicken and seafood in place of
pork, opening classic Malaysian-Chinese hawker dishes to a wider table.

## Signature dishes

In its roundup of inspectors' favourite dishes, MICHELIN singled out Bee Hwa's
**Penang Hokkien mee** (the island's prawn-noodle soup) and its **char kway teow**
— sauteed with minced garlic rather than lard, in a soy-sauce blend the guide
describes as spicy-sweet. The seafood-based hae mee broth is made fresh each day.
Together they are a compact tour of the hawker staples that define
[Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).

## Location and background

The cafe is at **10 Lebuh Dickens, George Town, 10050**, in the heart of the old
city. MICHELIN records that the owner started the business as a street stall in
1992 and now runs it with her son and daughter — a family operation that has grown
from a roadside pitch into a recognised sit-down coffeeshop over three decades.

## Visiting

Bee Hwa Cafe is a cash-only coffeeshop open for breakfast and lunch, listed at
07:00 to 15:00 Monday to Saturday and closed on Sunday. As with most Penang
hawker spots, hours can shift and busy dishes may run out, so it is best to arrive
early and confirm the day before you plan a special trip.
