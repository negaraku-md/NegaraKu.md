Peranakan cooking is labour-intensive, recipe-guarded and deeply domestic, which
is why a good restaurant version feels like a privilege. Bibik's Kitchen, on
Jalan Sri Bahari, earns that standing with a MICHELIN Guide
[Bib Gourmand](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## What it is

Bibik's Kitchen is a Peranakan restaurant in George Town, Penang, listed among
the Bib Gourmand entries of the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang. For the
2024 selection it was promoted from MICHELIN Selected to Bib Gourmand — a value
recommendation rather than a star. "Bibik" is an honorific for older Peranakan
women, and the dining room leans into the theme with classic Nyonya motifs.

## The cooking

The menu runs on the owner's heirloom family recipes — the heart of
[Nyonya Peranakan cuisine](/food-lifestyle/nyonya-peranakan-cuisine). MICHELIN
recommends the jiu hu char (stir-fried shredded jicama and cuttlefish) and the
loh bak, and notes that the dishes are slightly less spicy than their
counterparts elsewhere. Portions come in three sizes, which makes the table easy
to share.

## Where to find it

The restaurant is at 73 Jalan Sri Bahari, George Town, 10050, within the
heritage district's wider [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food) map. It
opens Tuesday to Sunday and closes on Mondays.

## A family table

The appeal is its lineage: a kitchen cooking one community's home recipes, kept
in the family rather than modernised into something unrecognisable. That
authenticity is what MICHELIN's inspectors rewarded.

## Visiting note

Bibik's Kitchen is closed on Mondays and handles its own reservations, so
contact the restaurant directly to book a table.
