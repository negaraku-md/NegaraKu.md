Tom yum is Thai by birth, but at a hawker stall near George Town's old Esplanade
it turns an unexpected shade of green — and that twist has carried Ghee Lian onto
Penang's MICHELIN map.

## A Bib Gourmand noodle stall

Ghee Lian is listed as a **MICHELIN Bib Gourmand** in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala
Lumpur & Penang selection, the award for good quality at good value. It is a
street-food stall with a tight, confident menu: just three noodle dishes, built
around one that few other kitchens attempt.

## The signature green tom yum

The dish that made Ghee Lian's name is its **green tom yum** noodle bowl — a fresh,
herb-green take on the usually red-orange Thai soup. MICHELIN says it "hits the
right balance of sour and spicy," and praises the crispy-coated fish topping and
the large, richly savoury prawns. Alongside it the stall serves a noodle soup and
a plate of fried noodles, rounding out a short menu that keeps the focus on the
specialty.

## Location and background

Ghee Lian operates from **Lot No. 39 of the Medan Renong food court** (Padang Kota
Lama), 4 Jalan Tun Syed Sheh Barakhbah, George Town, 10200 — a hawker complex near
the seafront Esplanade. Finding the right stall within the court is part of the
experience; MICHELIN notes the green tom yum stall tends to draw the longest queue
in the evenings, a reliable signal you are in the right place among George Town's
wider [street-food scene](/food-lifestyle/pasar-malam-and-street-food).

## Visiting

This is a cash-friendly hawker stall rather than a restaurant, so expect communal
food-court seating. MICHELIN's listed hours are limited and lean to the evening,
and stall schedules in Penang change frequently, so confirm the opening days and
arrive early enough to beat the queue before the night's batch runs out.
