Brickfields is Kuala Lumpur's Little India, and the arrival of a nearly
century-old South Indian name fits the neighbourhood perfectly. MTR 1924, on
Jalan Thambipillay, is the city's outpost of an international vegetarian chain —
and the MICHELIN Guide has given it a **Bib Gourmand**, the mark for good food at
a fair price.

## A Bib Gourmand for South Indian vegetarian

MTR 1924 serves [South Indian vegetarian](/food-lifestyle/indian-malaysian-cuisine)
food and sits in MICHELIN's budget ($) tier. It joined the Guide's Kuala Lumpur
and Penang Bib Gourmand list as a new entry in 2025, placing a humble tiffin
house alongside the city's more elaborate restaurants on the strength of value
and consistency rather than fine-dining polish. For how the Guide operates in
Malaysia, see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## The tiffin classics

The menu is built on South Indian breakfast-and-snack staples done properly.
MICHELIN singles out the idli — soft, steamed rice cakes — the masala dosa, a
crisp crepe wrapped around a spiced potato curry, and the uddin vada, a savoury
black-lentil fritter. Daily specials and set "mini meals" round out the
offering, making it an easy place to assemble a full vegetarian spread without
much ceremony.

## Background

By MICHELIN's account, MTR 1924 is the Kuala Lumpur branch of an international
chain that began almost 100 years ago and now has outlets around the world; this
particular outpost has been popular with locals since 2017. The restaurant's
name and that timeline are widely read as a nod to Bangalore's famous Mavalli
Tiffin Room, founded in 1924 — but the official MICHELIN listing does not name a
parent company, so the connection is best treated as reputed rather than
confirmed here.

## Visiting

This is an everyday vegetarian restaurant rather than a reservations destination,
well suited to breakfast, a dosa lunch or an early-evening tiffin. Being in
Brickfields, it is easy to reach by rail via KL Sentral. As with every Bib
Gourmand, the award is reviewed annually, so check the current MICHELIN selection
to confirm it still holds.
