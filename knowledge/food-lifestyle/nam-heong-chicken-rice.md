On Jalan Sultan, in the thick of Kuala Lumpur's old town, a plate of poached
chicken over fragrant rice has been drawing locals since before Merdeka. Nam
Heong Chicken Rice has been at it since 1938, and the MICHELIN Guide now lists
it with a Bib Gourmand.

## A household name since 1938

Nam Heong Chicken Rice (City Centre) is a specialist in
[Hainanese chicken rice](/food-lifestyle/hainanese-chicken-rice), recognised by
the MICHELIN Guide with a **Bib Gourmand** — its mark of good quality cooking at
good value. "Since 1938," the guide writes, "this household name has been
hugely popular among the locals for its famous Hainanese chicken rice." It is
listed in the $ (least expensive) band and flagged as family friendly.

## What to order

The choice that defines a visit is the chicken itself. The guide describes two
birds: a **"veggie farm" chicken**, leaner and firmer, and a **free-range**
option that is fattier and softer. Beyond the chicken, the barbecue meats are
bestsellers — the roast pork belly comes with crispy skin and alternating layers
of fat and lean — and the guide suggests ordering the crisp, juicy **Ipoh bean
sprouts** on the side.

## Visiting note

The City Centre shop is on Jalan Sultan in the old town and opens daily, roughly
from mid-morning to late evening. It is a plain, inexpensive eatery that manages
its own bookings. It is one of the budget-friendly stalwarts on the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) Bib Gourmand
list, which is refreshed each year.
