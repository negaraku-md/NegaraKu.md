Potager is a contemporary French restaurant tucked into Bamboo Hills, a
landscaped enclave off the DUKE highway in the Segambut district of Kuala
Lumpur. It is recognised in the MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang.

## What it is

The MICHELIN Guide classifies Potager as French Contemporary and lists it at a
`$$$$` price level. Its address is P11, Bamboo Hills, off DUKE, in Segambut — a
"lush green oasis outside the city centre," in the Guide's words, rather than a
city-centre dining room. It is an offshoot of the team behind Entier French
Dining, and sits among the city's established
[fine-dining](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia) addresses.

## MICHELIN recognition

Potager appears in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
2026 selection, where the published full list places it in the **"Selected"**
category — a form of MICHELIN recognition that sits below the one-star level.
Secondary reporting notes it had earlier carried the MICHELIN "Plate" (now
styled "Selected") distinction in 2024 and 2025. Because MICHELIN reassesses its
selections every year, the current distinction should be checked on the official
listing before relying on it.

## The food

The kitchen's defining trait is classic French technique applied to premium
produce. The MICHELIN Guide describes meticulously crafted **set menus** and
singles out a couple of hallmarks of the experience: a generously stocked cheese
trolley, and a dessert presented as a blind tasting of a sorbet quenelle. The
Guide also records an interesting wine list among the restaurant's facilities.

## The chef

Potager is led by chef **Masashi Horiuchi**, a native of Fukuoka, Japan, who —
per the MICHELIN Guide — worked in Europe for years before opening the
restaurant. Secondary reporting describes Potager as his project following his
work with Entier French Dining in Kuala Lumpur. The MICHELIN listing names "Chef
Masashi" in photo captions rather than in its description text, so the full
attribution is best confirmed against the restaurant's own material.

## Visiting

Potager lies outside the city centre and offers on-site parking, with the
MICHELIN Guide marking it as a date-night destination. Reported opening hours
differ between listing versions, and fine-dining menus, prices and distinctions
all change over time, so it is best to confirm the current set-menu format,
service times and reservation details with the restaurant before visiting.
