Bak kut teh rewards patience, and Ah Hei's day starts long before its first
customer: the shop sets its soup going at around 5am. The result is a dark,
herbal pork-rib broth that has earned the Pudu institution a MICHELIN **Bib
Gourmand** — the Guide's badge for good food that does not empty your wallet.

## A 30-year Bib Gourmand soup shop

Ah Hei has been simmering [bak kut teh](/food-lifestyle/bak-kut-teh) in central
Kuala Lumpur for roughly three decades. The MICHELIN Guide lists it under
Malaysian cuisine in the budget ($) tier and awards it a Bib Gourmand, and the
inspectors have gone a step further and profiled it in a "Behind the Bib"
feature. The Bib Gourmand is distinct from the Guide's stars: it singles out
value, not luxury — fitting for a herbal-soup specialist. For context on how the
Guide works here, see the
[MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia).

## The soup, and what goes in it

The broth is made fresh each morning from a blend of Chinese herbs — the listing
cites 16, a MICHELIN feature says about 15. It may look a touch dark, but, as the
Guide puts it, the colour belies a soup rich in aroma and flavour, with the
warming, restorative quality Malaysians associate with a good bak kut teh. The
standard bowl centres on pork spareribs, and diners build from there: tripe,
intestine and belly for the adventurous, plus mushrooms and deep-fried tofu skin
to soak up the liquor. MICHELIN's feature also points to the kampung chicken with
ginger and sesame oil, eggplant with belacan, and braised pork belly as dishes
worth ordering alongside.

## Where it is

Ah Hei sits at 33A Medan Imbi, in Pudu — a dense, old-KL neighbourhood where
hawker institutions cluster. It is a modest, no-frills shop rather than a
destination dining room, which is rather the point of a Bib Gourmand.

## Visiting

This is a daytime operation: it typically opens in the morning and closes by
mid-afternoon, and is usually shut on Wednesdays. Some MICHELIN listings note it
is cash only, so carry ringgit and arrive earlier rather than later, since
popular cuts sell out. Because the Bib Gourmand is reassessed each year, a glance
at the current MICHELIN selection will confirm the award still stands.
