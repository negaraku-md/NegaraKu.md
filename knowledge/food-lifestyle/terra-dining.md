Terra Dining is a fine-dining restaurant in the Taman Tun Dr Ismail (TTDI)
neighbourhood of Kuala Lumpur, recognised with one MICHELIN star in the
MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang 2026 selection.

## What it is

Terra Dining sits at 91 Jalan Aminuddin Baki in TTDI, a residential-commercial
quarter on the western side of Kuala Lumpur. The MICHELIN Guide classifies its
cooking as Malaysian and contemporary, and lists it at a `$$$` price level with
counter seating. It was awarded one MICHELIN star in the
[MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang 2026](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
selection, announced at a ceremony on 11 November 2025, where it was one of two
new one-star entrants alongside Akar — both TTDI restaurants that had been in
the Guide's "Selected" category the previous year. MICHELIN revises its
selections each year, so the distinction reflects the current edition rather
than a permanent status.

## The food

The restaurant's signature is a French-leaning **11-course tasting menu** built
on Malaysian produce. According to the MICHELIN Guide, self-taught chef Chong
"uses Malaysian produce to imbue his French-leaning 11-course tasting menu" with
local flavour. Two dishes the Guide highlights show the approach: a lobster
bisque made with a smoky oil and drawing on the sour, aromatic profile of
[asam laksa](/food-lifestyle/laksa), and a beurre blanc reworked as a creamy
turmeric-and-coconut-milk curry in the style of *masak lemak*. The meal comes
with curated tea pairings — the Guide suggests asking the chef about the tea
tasting notes — and wine pairings are offered in three- or five-glass options.

## The setting

Terra Dining is an intimate, counter-focused room rather than a large dining
hall; the MICHELIN Guide marks it as suited to date nights and solo dining, with
air conditioning and credit cards accepted. It is one of several KL fine-dining
rooms that has drawn MICHELIN attention in recent editions, part of a wider
map of [fine dining in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/fine-dining-malaysia).

## The chef

The kitchen is led by a chef the MICHELIN Guide identifies only as
"self-taught Chef Chong." Beyond the self-taught description and the French
technique evident in the menu, the Guide does not publish a detailed biography,
so further career details should be confirmed against a primary source.

## Visiting

The MICHELIN Guide lists Terra Dining as open Tuesday to Saturday for dinner and
closed on Sunday and Monday, with bookings handled by the restaurant directly
rather than through the Guide's website. Because fine-dining hours, menus and
pricing change frequently — and because MICHELIN distinctions are reassessed
annually — it is worth confirming the current tasting-menu format, prices and
reservation details with the restaurant before planning a visit.
