In a shoplot in Taman Tun Dr Ismail, the smell of freshly baked bread gives
Leen's away before the sign does. The restaurant began the quiet way — a Syrian
chef delivering home-cooked meals — and grew into one of the neighbourhood's
most talked-about tables, now carrying a MICHELIN Guide Bib Gourmand.

## A home kitchen that won a Bib Gourmand

Leen's serves Middle Eastern food in Kuala Lumpur's TTDI district. The MICHELIN
Guide recognises it with a **Bib Gourmand**, the distinction it reserves for
good quality cooking at good value (one notch of recognition that sits apart
from the Michelin star). By the guide's account, "after delivering home-cooked
meals, the Syrian chef opened his brick-and-mortar shop," and the place soon
became a local favourite. The food, it adds, is "unmistakably Middle Eastern,"
full of bold colours and flavours, with the aromas of baking filling the space.
It is listed in the $$ price band and flagged as good for groups.

## What to order

The dish most associated with Leen's is its **smoked hummus infused with burnt
chilli oil**, which the guide calls mildly spicy and very smoky — a twist on the
usual smooth chickpea purée. The other signature is **kebab khashkhash**, lamb
skewers served in a sharp, spicy poppy-seed sauce. Freshly baked breads round
out the table, which is how the room comes to smell the way it does.

## Where it is and when to go

Leen's is at 1F, 136A Jalan Burhanuddin Helmi, Taman Tun Dr Ismail, Kuala
Lumpur. It opens evenings on weekdays and adds a lunch service at the weekend.
The restaurant manages its own bookings, so contact it directly rather than
through the guide. It is air-conditioned and accepts credit cards.

## Visiting note

Leen's is one of a growing number of Kuala Lumpur eateries in the
[MICHELIN Guide Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia) selection
that reward everyday, good-value cooking rather than fine-dining formality. The
Bib Gourmand list is refreshed each year, so it is worth checking the current
edition before you plan a trip around it.
