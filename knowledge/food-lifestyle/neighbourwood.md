Not every Penang MICHELIN address is on the island. **Neighbourwood** sits in
Bukit Mertajam, on the mainland, and holds a **Bib Gourmand** — MICHELIN's award
for good-quality, good-value cooking — for a relaxed brand of contemporary
European food that rewards the short hop across the water.

## A neighbourhood table with a Bib Gourmand

MICHELIN tells the restaurant's origin plainly: a nearby resident opened this
cosy spot so his neighbours could enjoy European fare with Asian influences. The
result is unpretentious rather than formal, and the Bib Gourmand recognises the
balance it strikes — careful cooking at a fair (MICHELIN $$) price.

## What to order

The kitchen cooks **European Contemporary** food with Asian touches alongside
**grilled dishes**, and the **menu changes every six weeks**, so there is reason
to return. A few signatures stay on and are built for sharing. Two things to seek
out: the **golden, crumbly house-baked sourdough**, offered in several flavours,
and a dessert list running to more than ten options.

## Location: mainland Penang

Neighbourwood is at **3427 Jalan Kulim, Taman Bukit Mas, Bukit Mertajam, Seberang
Perai, 14000**. Seberang Perai is the mainland half of Penang state — the side
facing George Town across the channel, reached by the Penang bridges or the
ferry. It is a useful anchor for anyone eating their way beyond the island.

## Planning a visit

Because the menu rotates on a six-week cycle, what you ate last time may have
moved on — part of the appeal. Confirm current hours and any reservation policy
with the restaurant before you travel over. For the bigger picture, see our
overview of the [MICHELIN Guide in Malaysia](/food-lifestyle/michelin-guide-malaysia)
and the guide to [Penang food](/food-lifestyle/penang-food).
