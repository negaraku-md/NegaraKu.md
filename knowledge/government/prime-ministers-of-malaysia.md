Malaysia's head of government is the **Prime Minister** — the leader who commands
the confidence of the majority of the Dewan Rakyat and is appointed by the
[Yang di-Pertuan Agong](/government/yang-di-pertuan-agong). Since independence in
1957 the office has passed through **ten premierships held by nine people**.

## The ten premierships

| # | Prime Minister | In office | Party / coalition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tunku Abdul Rahman | 31 Aug 1957 – 22 Sep 1970 | UMNO · Alliance |
| 2 | Abdul Razak Hussein | 22 Sep 1970 – 14 Jan 1976 | UMNO · founded Barisan Nasional (1973) |
| 3 | Hussein Onn | 15 Jan 1976 – 16 Jul 1981 | UMNO · Barisan Nasional |
| 4 | Mahathir Mohamad *(1st term)* | 16 Jul 1981 – 31 Oct 2003 | UMNO · Barisan Nasional |
| 5 | Abdullah Ahmad Badawi | 31 Oct 2003 – 3 Apr 2009 | UMNO · Barisan Nasional |
| 6 | Najib Razak | 3 Apr 2009 – 9 May 2018 | UMNO · Barisan Nasional |
| 7 | Mahathir Mohamad *(2nd term)* | 10 May 2018 – 1 Mar 2020 | Pakatan Harapan |
| 8 | Muhyiddin Yassin | 1 Mar 2020 – 16 Aug 2021 | Perikatan Nasional |
| 9 | Ismail Sabri Yaakob | 21 Aug 2021 – 24 Nov 2022 | Barisan Nasional |
| 10 | Anwar Ibrahim | 24 Nov 2022 – present | Pakatan Harapan · unity (MADANI) government |

## Two eras

For the first six premierships — a little over six decades — the office was held
without interruption by **UMNO** leaders, governing first through the **Alliance**
and, from 1973, the broader **[Barisan Nasional](/malaysia/barisan-nasional)**
coalition. The **[14th general election in 2018](/malaysia/2018-change-of-government)**
ended that run, producing the first change of federal government since
independence.

The years since have been the most fluid in the office's history: **four
premierships in under eight years**, moving across Pakatan Harapan, Perikatan
Nasional and Barisan Nasional, before settling on Anwar Ibrahim's unity
government from November 2022.

## About this list

Tenure dates follow the Prime Minister's Office and the Perdana Leadership
Foundation. Where an outgoing Prime Minister stayed on in a caretaker capacity —
as Ismail Sabri Yaakob did between Parliament's dissolution and the swearing-in
of his successor — the end date shown is the date the office actually changed
hands. This is an early draft; the individual premierships are summarised
neutrally, and fuller articles on each are planned.
