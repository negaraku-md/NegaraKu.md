There is no single national ferry line in Malaysia. Reaching an island means knowing which short crossing, which jetty and which operator serves it — and, on the east coast, whether the sea is even open that month.

## How do you get to Penang island by ferry?

The Penang crossing is a long-running passenger ferry that links **Butterworth** on the mainland with **Pengkalan Weld** in George Town, and is run by the **Penang Port Commission**.

Ferries run daily from 06:30 to 23:30, at regular intervals through the day (more frequent during peak commuting hours). The current boats carry pedestrians, bicycles and motorcycles only — not cars. Fares are among the cheapest public transport in Malaysia:

| Passenger / vehicle | Fare |
| --- | --- |
| Adult (foot passenger) | RM 2.00 |
| Child (5-11) | RM 1.00 |
| Child under 4 / OKU | Free |
| Motorcycle | RM 2.50 |
| Bicycle | RM 2.00 |

You can pay by Touch 'n Go, at the counter, or with an online ticket. Many travellers also reach the island by road, via the Penang Bridge or the Sultan Abdul Halim Muadzam Shah (Second) Bridge; the ferry is a scenic and commuter option rather than the only link.

## How do ferries to Langkawi work?

Langkawi has no bridge, so ferries and flights are the only ways in. Regular passenger ferries reach **Kuah Jetty** on Langkawi from two mainland ports:

- **Kuala Kedah** — journey of about 1 hour 45 minutes, adult fare around RM23 one way.
- **Kuala Perlis** — journey of about 1 hour 15 minutes, adult fare around RM18 one way, the shorter and usually cheaper option.

Both run about 10 sailings a day, roughly every 45 minutes. The largest operator is **Langkawi Ferry Line Ventures**, which asks passengers to be inside the terminal at least 60 minutes before departure. There is also a cross-border service from **Satun in Thailand** (around three sailings a day, about 1 hour 15 minutes), which likewise arrives at Kuah Jetty.

## What about Pangkor, Labuan and the other islands?

Each has its own short, dedicated crossing:

- **Pangkor** (Perak) — ferries from the Lumut jetty, a crossing of about 30 minutes.
- **Labuan** — frequent passenger ferries connect the federal territory with **Menumbok** in Sabah (about 30 minutes), and vehicle (RoRo) ferries run the same route for those bringing a car.
- **Tioman** — reached from **Mersing** (Johor) by Bluewater Express, which serves island jetties such as Tekek, ABC (Air Batang), Paya, Genting and Salang.
- **Redang** — boats from **Merang Jetty** in Terengganu (about 45 minutes to an hour).
- **Perhentian** — boats from **Kuala Besut Jetty** in Terengganu (about 45 minutes to an hour).

These are local operations, so schedules, fares and jetties change; always confirm the day's sailings with the operator before travelling.

## When does the monsoon close the islands?

This is the single biggest planning factor on the east coast. The **northeast monsoon** brings turbulent seas from the South China Sea, and most island resorts and boat services shut for it.

In Terengganu — home to Redang and Perhentian — hotels, resorts and guesthouses typically suspend operations from about **October to February**, using the downtime for repairs. The state does not order the closure; it is a voluntary safety decision by operators, "for the safety of tourists, especially due to the uncertainty of weather conditions and turbulent seas," as the state tourism director put it. Tioman ferries from Mersing similarly thin out or stop for days at a time during the monsoon months. Exact closure and reopening dates vary by island and operator.

The west coast (Penang, Langkawi, Pangkor) is sheltered from this and runs year-round, though rough-weather cancellations can still happen.

## What's next

Confirm exact times and fares on the operator or port website on the day you travel, and for east-coast islands check whether the resort and boat you want are open before booking. If timing or the monsoon rules out a ferry, compare it against the domestic flight network, which reaches Langkawi, Tioman and other island airports year-round.
