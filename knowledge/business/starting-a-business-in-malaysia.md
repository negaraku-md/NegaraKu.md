Every business in Malaysia starts at the same door: the Companies Commission of Malaysia, known everywhere by its Malay initials **SSM** (Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia). What changes is which structure you register, which portal you use, and what the registration costs you — in money and in personal risk.

This is the map. Each route below has its own detailed guide; start here to pick the right one.

## Step 1 — Choose a structure

The single most consequential decision is whether you want **limited liability**. A sole proprietorship and a conventional partnership are cheap and simple, but the owner is personally liable without limit for every business debt. A company (Sdn Bhd) or a limited liability partnership (LLP) puts a legal wall between you and the business.

| Structure | Governing law | Registered on | SSM fee | Minimum people | Limited liability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sole proprietorship | Registration of Businesses Act 1956 | EzBiz | RM30/yr (personal name), RM60/yr (trade name) | 1 owner | No |
| Partnership | Registration of Businesses Act 1956 | EzBiz | RM60/yr (trade name) | 2–20 partners | No |
| LLP | LLP Act 2012 | MyLLP | RM500 | 2 partners | Yes |
| Sdn Bhd | Companies Act 2016 | MyCoID | RM1,000 | 1 director, 1 shareholder | Yes |

Sole proprietorships and partnerships are open **only to Malaysian citizens and permanent residents aged 18 or above**. Foreigners must use an LLP or a Sdn Bhd. See [business structures compared](/en/business/business-structures-malaysia) and, for the closest head-to-head, [sole proprietorship vs Sdn Bhd](/en/business/sole-prop-vs-sdn-bhd).

## Step 2 — Register with SSM

**Sole proprietorship or partnership.** Register online through the **EzBiz** portal under the Registration of Businesses Act 1956 (Act 197). The law gives you **no later than 30 days from the date the business commenced** to register — the clock starts when you start trading, not when you apply. A personal name (the name on your MyKad) costs RM30 a year; an invented trade name costs RM60 a year and needs SSM's approval. The certificate can be issued within about an hour of payment. Full walkthrough: [registering on EzBiz](/en/business/register-enterprise-ezbiz).

**Private limited company (Sdn Bhd).** Incorporate through the **MyCoID** portal under the Companies Act 2016 (Act 777) for a flat **RM1,000**. You need at least one director who **ordinarily resides in Malaysia**, at least one shareholder, and you must appoint a qualified **company secretary within 30 days** of incorporation. SSM issues a Notice of Registration, usually within one business day. See [registering a Sdn Bhd](/en/business/register-sdn-bhd).

**Limited liability partnership (LLP).** Register through the **MyLLP** portal under the LLP Act 2012 for **RM500**. An LLP needs at least two partners and a **compliance officer** who is a Malaysian citizen or permanent resident. See [registering an LLP](/en/business/register-llp-malaysia).

Trading without registering an unincorporated business is an offence under the Registration of Businesses Act 1956, carrying a fine of up to **RM50,000** or two years' imprisonment.

## Step 3 — The steps registration does not cover

SSM registration makes your business a recognised legal entity. It does **not** license the activity or square you with the tax authorities. After you register:

- **Licences.** Most businesses need a **premise licence** and **signboard licence** from the local council, and many need sector permits (food, wholesale/retail trade, manufacturing). Start at the [business licence overview](/en/business/business-licence-malaysia).
- **Income tax.** Register the business or company for income tax with the Inland Revenue Board (**LHDN**) and obtain a tax file number.
- **Employees.** Once you hire staff, you must register as an employer with the **EPF (KWSP)** and **SOCSO/EIS (PERKESO)** and contribute for each employee.
- **Bank account.** Open a business or corporate bank account in the entity's name so business funds stay separate from personal ones.

## What's next

If you have already been trading, register now — the 30-day deadline for an unincorporated business runs from commencement, and exposure grows with every unregistered contract. If you are still choosing, decide the liability question first: read [sole proprietorship vs Sdn Bhd](/en/business/sole-prop-vs-sdn-bhd), then follow the specific registration guide for the structure you pick.
