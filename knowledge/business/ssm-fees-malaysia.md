Three separate fee schedules, three separate statutes, and no single page on SSM's site
that puts them side by side. Company fees come from the Companies Regulations 2017; LLP
fees from the LLP Regulations 2012; business registration fees from SSM's own guidelines
under the Registration of Businesses Act 1956.

All figures below are the prescribed amounts. Late-lodgement penalties and service
provider charges sit on top.

## Businesses — Registration of Businesses Act 1956

These are **annual** rates. You select a registration period of one to five years and pay
the rate for each year.

| Matter | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Sole proprietorship using the personal name on the identity card | RM30 per year |
| Sole proprietorship or partnership using a trade name | RM60 per year |
| Registration of each branch | RM5 per year |
| Business information print | RM10 |

Renewal is charged at the same rates. Registration runs for a period not exceeding five
years (s.5(3) of ROBA 1956), and renewal likewise not exceeding five years at each
renewal (s.5A(2)).

**These fees apply in Peninsular Malaysia and the Federal Territory of Labuan only.**
Section 1(2) of ROBA 1956 confines the Act to Peninsular Malaysia. Sabah and Sarawak run
separate regimes with separate fees.

## Late lodgement penalties

Practice Directive 1/2017, revised 1 October 2024, paragraph 17. The bands begin after
**seven days** of default and rise with the length of the delay:

| Entity | Penalty bands |
| --- | --- |
| Private company | RM50 / RM100 / RM150 / RM200 |
| Public or foreign company | RM150 / RM250 / RM300 / RM500 |

Paragraph 18 gives the Registrar a remission power. There is no published late-lodgement
scale for beneficial ownership filings — do not assume one exists.

## Processing times SSM actually publishes

- **Business registration certificate** — within one hour of the payment transaction for
  a new registration, and within fifteen minutes for a renewal, which is auto-approved
  when done through EzBiz.
- **Foreign company** — notice of registration within one working day of a compliant
  submission.
- **Certificates and business info** — downloadable for 90 days from the approval date
  through the SSM4U portal. After that you pay again.

SSM does not publish an equivalent service standard for company incorporation or for LLP
registration.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating the RM1,000 incorporation fee as the cost of starting a company.** It is one
  line. The first year also carries a company secretary, a registered office and the
  annual return.
- **Quoting the ROB fees as one-off.** RM30 and RM60 are per year of the registration
  period selected.
- **Assuming a name reservation can be renewed indefinitely.** The cap is 180 days.
- **Paying RM50 to reserve a name you intend to use immediately.** Direct incorporation
  folds the name check into the RM1,000 application.
- **Budgeting a branch registration at the RM1,000 rate.** A foreign company branch
  starts at RM5,000 and reaches RM70,000, and RM70,000 is also the flat rate where there
  is no share capital.
- **Missing that unaudited financial statements are cheaper to lodge.** RM20 against
  RM50 — small, but it is a real consequence of qualifying for audit exemption.

## What's next

Price the recurring line, not the formation line. For a private company that is RM150
plus RM50 a year before anyone's professional fee; for an LLP, RM200; for an enterprise,
RM30 or RM60. Then check whether your entity is in Sabah or Sarawak, because none of the
ROB figures on this page apply there.
