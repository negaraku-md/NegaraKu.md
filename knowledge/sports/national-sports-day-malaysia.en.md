Every Saturday of the second week of October, sports fields, parks and halls across Malaysia are meant to be packed — that is the intent behind National Sports Day.

## What is National Sports Day?

National Sports Day (HSN) is an annual celebration introduced by the Ministry of Youth and Sports (KBS) in 2015. It is celebrated on the Saturday of the second week of October and carried out simultaneously at the district, state and national levels.

Unlike championships such as the Malaysia Games (SUKMA), which focus on high-performance athletes, HSN is designed for ordinary people. Its emphasis is *mass participation* — broad involvement by the public across all ages and backgrounds.

## Why does it exist?

HSN grew out of the aspiration to make Malaysia a "Sporting Nation". The idea is simple: sport should not just be watched, but practised as part of everyday life.

The need is real. Based on the Malaysia Sports Culture Index 2022 (IBSM'22) referenced by KBS, about 52% of Malaysians are not involved in any sporting activity, with higher rates of inactivity among women, students and senior citizens. HSN aims to close that gap by encouraging active habits on a broad scale.

The main objectives of HSN include:

- Cultivating sport as a way of life for the people.
- Encouraging a healthy and active lifestyle for all ages.
- Generating mass participation across races and communities.
- Supporting Malaysia's goal of becoming a sporting nation.

## When and how is it celebrated?

The core date is the Saturday of the second week of October. For example, HSN 2025 was celebrated on 11 October 2025, while HSN 2026 is scheduled for 10 October 2026.

Since 2019, KBS has expanded that single day into **National Sports Month (BSN)**, running throughout October to give more room for programmes across the country.

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Introduced | 2015 |
| Day of celebration | Saturday of the second week of October |
| Organiser | Ministry of Youth and Sports (KBS) |
| Levels | District, state and national (simultaneous) |
| Expansion (since 2019) | National Sports Month throughout October |

By its 11th edition in 2025, KBS reported that the HSN celebration had recorded around 31 million public participations through more than 71,000 programmes and activities over the course of its implementation.

## Who can take part?

Everyone. HSN is organised through schools, communities, organisations, employers and local authorities. Activities typically include runs, aerobics, folk sports, traditional games and friendly competitions that prioritise participation over winning.

The HSN secretariat is run by the National Department of Youth and Sports (JBSN) under the Ministry of Youth and Sports. Participation details and official contacts can be checked through the official portal harisukannegara.my.

## What's next

To take part, check the current HSN date and theme on the official portal harisukannegara.my or contact your district Youth and Sports Office. Schools and employers can register their own programmes under the National Sports Month celebration in October.
