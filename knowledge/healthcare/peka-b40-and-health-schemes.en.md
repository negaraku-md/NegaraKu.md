Many people have heard the name "PeKa B40," but assumptions about it vary
widely — some think it covers all hospital costs, some confuse it with
mySalam, and some ignore it altogether because they think a separate
application is needed. All three assumptions are wrong. PeKa B40 has a
clear and limited scope — and knowing that scope means knowing when to
use it, and when to look for other help.

## What PeKa B40 Is

PeKa B40, or Skim Peduli Kesihatan untuk Kumpulan B40 (B40 Health Care
Scheme), is a government initiative under the Ministry of Health Malaysia
(KKM), operated day-to-day by **ProtectHealth Corporation Sdn Bhd**. Its
focus is the prevention and treatment of non-communicable diseases (NCD)
among low-income groups, through early screening and financial aid when
treatment is needed.

It is not insurance, and it is not a monthly cash payment. It is a
package of four specific forms of aid accessed at government health
facilities.

## Four Components of Aid

| Aid | What it covers | Limit/Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health screening | Physical examination, mental health screening, blood and urine tests, breast/prostate examination | Free, no out-of-pocket cost |
| Medical equipment aid | Heart stents, hearing aids, pacemakers, joint/bone prostheses, intraocular lenses, wheelchairs and similar items | Up to **RM20,000** |
| Cancer treatment incentive | Payment upon completing cancer treatment at a KKM hospital | **RM1,000** — RM300 at the start of treatment, RM700 after completion |
| Transport allowance | Covers travel costs to hospital for the treatment above | Up to **RM500** (Peninsular Malaysia) or **RM1,000** (Sabah/Sarawak/Labuan) |

The last two forms of aid — the cancer incentive and the transport
allowance — only apply to recipients who have already claimed medical
equipment aid or cancer treatment; they are not stand-alone aid.

Medical equipment aid and the cancer incentive are only disbursed at
**KKM hospitals**, through a referral from the treating doctor. Free
screening, on the other hand, can be done at registered private or
government clinics that display the PeKa B40 logo.

## Who Is Eligible, and How It's Verified

PeKa B40 eligibility does not involve a separate application form. It is
verified automatically based on two conditions:

1. A Malaysian citizen who is a **recipient of Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah
   (STR)**, or the registered spouse of such a recipient.
2. Aged **40 and above**.

Anyone who meets both conditions is eligible without needing to register
specially. Eligibility status can be checked by entering a MyKad number
on the official portal **kelayakan.pekab40.com.my**. Once eligible,
bring your MyKad to the relevant clinic or hospital for verification at
the counter.

## PeKa B40 Is Not mySalam — Don't Confuse the Two

This is the most common source of confusion. **mySalam** is a separate
scheme — a takaful protection scheme operated by Great Eastern Takaful
Berhad under the Ministry of Finance Malaysia, not KKM/ProtectHealth. Its
basic eligibility is also based on STR recipient status, but its aid is
entirely different:

| | PeKa B40 | mySalam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operator | ProtectHealth (on behalf of KKM) | Great Eastern Takaful (on behalf of MOF) |
| Nature of aid | Screening + equipment + treatment incentive | Daily ward allowance + lump-sum critical illness payout |
| Government hospital ward allowance | None | Yes — daily allowance while admitted, capped per year |
| Critical illness (one-off payment) | None | Yes — one-off lump-sum payment once confirmed |

Both schemes can be claimed at the same time because they cover
different things — one for screening and equipment, the other for
allowances while admitted to a ward or once a critical illness is
confirmed. Assuming one already covers the other means missing out on
aid you would otherwise be entitled to claim.

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming PeKa B40 covers all hospital costs.** It only covers the
  four specific components above, not general hospital bills.
- **Not claiming because of thinking an application must be made
  first.** Eligibility is automatic for STR recipients aged 40+; you
  only need to check your status and go to a registered facility with
  your MyKad.
- **Ignoring mySalam because of thinking you're already covered by PeKa
  B40.** Both schemes are separate and can be claimed at the same time.
- **Trying to claim equipment aid at a private clinic.** This component
  is only available at KKM hospitals through a doctor's referral.
