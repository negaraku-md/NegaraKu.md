A fever that won't break, a child due for an immunisation jab, or blood
pressure that needs monthly monitoring — and the first question that comes up
usually isn't "what's the treatment", but "which clinic do I go to". Private
clinics are fast but expensive. Government hospitals are crowded for minor
problems. And "government clinic" itself is actually not a single type of
facility — many people don't realise Klinik Kesihatan, Klinik Komuniti and
Klinik Pergigian are three separate facilities under the same roof.

## Klinik Kesihatan is not KKM's only facility

According to the official government portal, among the health facilities
provided by the Ministry of Health Malaysia (KKM) are Hospital, Klinik
Kesihatan, Klinik Komuniti and Klinik Pergigian — each playing a role in
providing "basic health services and comprehensive treatment to the community
including outpatient treatment, maternal and child health, mental health,
dental and others".

| Facility | General focus |
| --- | --- |
| Klinik Kesihatan | Comprehensive primary care — general outpatient, maternal & child, chronic disease |
| Klinik Komuniti | Separate facility under KKM, searchable via the official portal by location |
| Klinik Pergigian | Basic dental treatment, separate from Klinik Kesihatan |
| Hospital | Specialist treatment and more complex referral cases |

Klinik Kesihatan itself is where most day-to-day health matters should
start — not the hospital — because it is designed for a scope far wider than
just minor treatment.

## What is actually offered

Based on the official list of services that can be booked at KKM facilities,
the scope of Klinik Kesihatan covers:

- General outpatient treatment (fever, cough, minor wounds, and so on)
- Pregnant mothers' health, prenatal and postnatal care
- Immunisation and monitoring of child development
- Management of chronic diseases such as diabetes and high blood pressure
- Family planning services
- Smoking cessation services
- Influenza immunisation for senior citizens
- Pre-employment and pre-study health examinations
- Pre-marital screening
- Health examinations for prospective Hajj pilgrims
- Dental health examinations

This makes it an all-round facility for most routine health needs across a
person's lifetime — not just a place for "minor ailments".

## How to register and make an appointment

Since the appointment system was expanded to cover all KKM klinik kesihatan
services, the standard way to deal with Klinik Kesihatan is through the
**MySejahtera** app or website (mysejahtera.moh.gov.my). The process:

1. Select the household member who needs to be seen (yourself or a dependant).
2. Select the type of service required from the list provided.
3. Select a Klinik Kesihatan by nearby location.
4. Select an available date and time slot.
5. Complete personal details (phone number/email and identification card/
   passport number) and submit.

This booking service is free and can be accessed immediately, whether through
the mobile app (Google Play/App Store) or the web portal, and is open to both
citizens and non-citizens.

## PeKa B40: an additional layer at the same facility

For B40 households receiving Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR), or their spouses,
the **PeKa B40** scheme — administered by ProtectHealth Corporation Sdn Bhd on
behalf of KKM — provides free health screening at registered government
health facilities, alongside medical equipment assistance and cancer
treatment incentives for eligible cases. No special registration is required
for STR recipients who already qualify.

## The question of charges: why this article does not state a figure

Registration charges at Klinik Kesihatan are set centrally by the government
under the existing legislative framework — not at private market rates — and
this is what makes Klinik Kesihatan far more affordable than private clinics
for most basic treatment. However, the exact charge figure is not stated here
because it could not be directly verified against official KKM sources at the
time this article was written. Please check with the registration counter of
the relevant clinic for current rates and any exemptions that may apply to
particular categories of patient.

## Common mistakes

- **Going straight to the hospital for a problem Klinik Kesihatan can
  handle.** Fever, minor wounds and routine follow-up for a stable chronic
  disease usually do not require a hospital visit.
- **Not booking an appointment through MySejahtera and then being surprised
  by long queues.** The appointment system is now the standard route for
  most services.
- **Assuming Klinik Kesihatan, Klinik Komuniti and Klinik Pergigian are the
  same facility.** All three are listed separately by KKM, each with its own
  role.
- **Missing out on PeKa B40 benefits by assuming separate registration is
  needed.** Eligible STR recipients already qualify automatically, with no
  additional registration process.
