On 9 October 2026, Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim tabled
**Budget 2027** — a record **RM459.8 billion** spending plan the government
framed around three themes: easing the cost of living, cutting taxes, and
raising wages.

This page is a plain, non-partisan summary of what was announced and — just as
important — **when each measure actually takes effect**. One thing to hold onto
throughout: a budget speech is an *announcement*, not a law. The measures below
become binding only once the Finance Bill, the Supply (Budget) 2027 Act and any
enabling orders are passed by Parliament and gazetted. For how that process
works, see [the federal budget cycle](/economy/federal-budget).

## If you're an individual

- **Income tax is cut.** Resident individual income-tax rates are reduced by
  **one percentage point**. The general personal relief — unchanged since 2010 —
  rises from **RM9,000 to RM12,000**. Income above **RM1 million** is taxed at
  **30%**. Together the relief and rate changes are reported to leave several
  million taxpayers with more take-home pay. See
  [personal income tax rates](/taxation/personal-tax-rates) for the current
  bands (to be updated once the new schedule is gazetted).
- **EPF from age 18.** Every citizen will be **automatically registered as an
  EPF member upon turning 18**, to widen retirement coverage.
- **Cash aid.** **STR** rises to about **RM16 billion**, with **SARA** on top:
  up to **RM150 a month** (RM1,800 a year) for nine million recipients, plus a
  **RM100** payment twice a year (before Aidilfitri and around National Day) for
  13 million Malaysians aged 18 and above.

## If you're a worker

- **Minimum wage → RM2,000.** The national minimum wage rises from **RM1,700 to
  RM2,000 a month from June 2027**. **MSMEs with annual sales below RM50 million
  are exempted initially** to give them time to adjust.
- **A RM2,500 floor** applies to **semi-skilled workers and graduates**, to
  better match pay to qualifications.
- **Living wage in GLCs.** Government-linked companies and investment companies
  raise their living-wage benchmark from **RM3,100 to RM3,400**.
- **Gig workers.** The government will provide **EPF matching contributions of
  up to RM600 a year** (up to RM6,000 over a lifetime) for gig workers.
- **Expatriate spouses.** Spouses of Category I Employment Pass holders on a
  dependant pass will be **allowed to work from 1 January 2027**, subject to
  conditions.

## If you run a business or SME

- **SME income tax cut.** Small and medium enterprises get a **one-percentage-
  point cut** in income-tax rates (reported new rates of **14% and 16%**),
  benefiting around 300,000 businesses.
- **Capital allowances.** The qualifying value for **small-value assets** rises
  to **RM3,000 per asset**, and the overall claim limit for non-MSME companies
  rises to **RM30,000**. See [capital allowances](/taxation/capital-allowances).
- **Sales tax on machinery.** Manufacturers will be able to **reclaim sales tax**
  on machinery, spare parts and equipment bought from local traders or
  distributors.
- **Service tax on elderly care** is cut from **8% to 6%** from **1 January
  2027**, and care fees of up to **RM96,000 a year** are fully exempt — see
  [the Service Tax Act 2018](/taxation/service-tax-act-2018).
- **Stamp duty.** Opening a savings or current account is **fully stamp-duty
  exempt** from **1 January 2027** — see [stamp duty rates](/taxation/stamp-duty-rates).
- **Semiconductors.** Khazanah Nasional and InvestPenang will set up a
  **RM100 million fund** for semiconductor and advanced-manufacturing firms.

## Announced vs. in force

Everything here is **as announced on 9 October 2026**. None of it is law yet.
Tax changes take effect through the **Finance Bill** (and the relevant Year of
Assessment); spending is authorised by the **Supply (Budget) 2027 Act**; and
specific reliefs or rate changes are implemented through **gazetted orders
(P.U.(A))**. Dates and figures can shift between the speech and the final law —
so for anything you are relying on for planning or compliance, check the
**official Budget 2027 documents** at
[belanjawan.mof.gov.my](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/en) and the gazette.

## Commonly confused

**"Budget 2027 reformed SST / changed e-invoicing."** Not in this budget. A
broad SST reform and new e-invoicing measures were **pre-budget forecasts by
analysts**, not announcements in the speech. Treat them as expectations until
the government says otherwise.

**"The budget is approved on the day it's tabled."** No — it passes through
weeks of parliamentary debate and committee stages before becoming law. See
[the federal budget cycle](/economy/federal-budget).
