**Bahasa Malaysia:** Wilayah Persekutuan · **English:** Federal Territory · **中文:** 联邦直辖区

Wilayah Persekutuan is an area administered **directly by the Federal
Government**. This is why Kuala Lumpur, despite being the capital city, is
not a state — and has no Menteri Besar.

## In practice

Malaysia is made up of **13 states and 3 Wilayah Persekutuan**:

| Territory | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Kuala Lumpur** | Capital city and business hub |
| **Putrajaya** | Administrative centre of the Federal Government |
| **Labuan** | Offshore territory in Borneo, offshore financial centre |

Key differences from a state:

- **No Raja or Yang di-Pertua Negeri.** States have a Sultan, Raja or
  Yang di-Pertua Negeri as head of state; a Wilayah Persekutuan does not.
- **No Menteri Besar or Ketua Menteri.** Territory matters are handled at
  the federal level.
- **No Dewan Undangan Negeri.** Residents vote for Parliamentary seats, but
  there are no state elections because there is no DUN.
