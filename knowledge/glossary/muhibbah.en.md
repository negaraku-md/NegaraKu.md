**Bahasa Malaysia:** Muhibbah · **English:** Goodwill / inter-communal harmony · **中文:** 睦邻友好（族群和谐）

Say "semangat muhibbah" in Malaysia and almost everyone understands what it
means, even though few stop to think about where the word comes from. Its
origin is neither Malay nor any modern political term — it is rooted in an
Arabic word meaning *love*.

## From 'love' to 'unity'

Muhibbah comes from the Arabic word *maḥabbah* (محبة), which derives from the
root *ḥubb* — meaning love or affection. Kamus Dewan marks the Arabic origin
of this word and gives two interrelated meanings, as summarised in the table
below.

| Aspect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Root of the word** | From the Arabic *maḥabbah*, from *ḥubb* (love, affection) |
| **Dictionary meaning** | Kamus Dewan lists two meanings: (1) love, tender affection; (2) a feeling of friendship and warmth |
| **Everyday usage** | Refers to goodwill, harmony and friendship across races and religions |
| **Common phrases** | "Semangat muhibbah", "acara muhibbah", "hubungan muhibbah" |

So, from an original meaning centred on feelings of love and friendship, the
word has broadened into a wider social sense in Malaysia: it has become a
shorthand for goodwill and warm relations between different communities.

## 'Semangat muhibbah' in Malaysian discourse

In Malaysia's plural society, made up of various races and religions,
muhibbah is often referred to as a value that supports shared harmony. The
term commonly appears in official speeches, media coverage and programme names
— for example "acara muhibbah" (goodwill events) that bring together
neighbours or colleagues from different backgrounds, or open-house sessions
during festivities.

Academic studies also discuss the role of this concept in nation-building,
including the challenges and hopes in fostering harmony across communities.
Some writers distinguish muhibbah from mere *tolerance*: tolerance means
accepting the existence of others, whereas muhibbah carries a warmer tone,
namely active goodwill and openness.

It should be noted that muhibbah is a concept widely applied in public
discourse, and its meaning is often associated with principles of unity such
as those contained in the [Rukun Negara](/en/glossary/rukun-negara). This
article describes the term and its usage descriptively, without taking sides
with any particular political interpretation.

## Common mistakes

- **Equating muhibbah with tolerance alone.** Tolerance means accommodating
  differences; muhibbah carries a warmer meaning, namely active goodwill and
  friendship.
- **Assuming "muhibbah" is the standard spelling.** The Kamus Dewan spelling
  is *muhibah* (one b), although *muhibbah* (two b's) is more common in
  everyday usage and informal writing.
- **Thinking it is a modern political term.** The word is rooted in the Arabic
  for *love* and had long existed in Malay before it became a common term in
  the discourse of unity.
