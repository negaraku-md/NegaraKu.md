Muhyiddin Yassin (Mahiaddin bin Md Yassin, born 15 May 1947 in Muar, Johor) was
Malaysia's **eighth Prime Minister**. A long-serving UMNO politician and former
Menteri Besar of Johor, he held senior federal portfolios over several decades
and served as **Deputy Prime Minister from 2009 to 2015** under
[Najib Razak](/malaysia/najib-razak), before being removed from that post in
2015.

## From BERSATU to the premiership

In 2016 Muhyiddin **co-founded the party BERSATU** with
[Mahathir Mohamad](/malaysia/mahathir-mohamad), and became its president. BERSATU
was part of the Pakatan Harapan coalition that won the
[2018 general election](/malaysia/2018-change-of-government), and Muhyiddin
served as Home Minister.

He became Prime Minister after the **February 2020
[Sheraton Move](/malaysia/sheraton-move-2020)**, when a bloc of MPs left Pakatan
Harapan and the government lost its majority. The Yang di-Pertuan Agong
determined that Muhyiddin was likely to command majority support, and he was
**sworn in on 1 March 2020**, heading a new **Perikatan Nasional** government.

## Premiership through the pandemic (2020–2021)

Muhyiddin's government governed through the COVID-19 pandemic, overseeing the
**Movement Control Order** from March 2020 and a nationwide **state of emergency**
from January to August 2021. He **resigned on 16 August 2021** after losing his
parliamentary majority, and was succeeded by
[Ismail Sabri Yaakob](/malaysia/ismail-sabri-yaakob). His tenure of roughly
17 months is among the shortest of any Malaysian Prime Minister.

## After the premiership

Muhyiddin remained BERSATU president and led the Perikatan Nasional coalition in
opposition after the [2022 general election](/malaysia/2022-general-election). As
a matter of public record, he was later **charged in connection with corruption
and money-laundering allegations**; the status of those charges shifted across
2023–2025 and, at the time of writing, the case remained before the courts. This
article does not recount the detail of those proceedings.
