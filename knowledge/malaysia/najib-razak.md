Najib Razak (Mohd Najib bin Abdul Razak, born 23 July 1953) was Malaysia's
**sixth Prime Minister**. Born into a political family — the son of the second
Prime Minister, Abdul Razak Hussein, and nephew of the third, Hussein Onn — he
entered Parliament young and rose through UMNO to hold senior cabinet posts,
including Defence and Education, before becoming Deputy Prime Minister in 2004.

## Premiership (2009–2018)

Najib took office on **3 April 2009**, succeeding Abdullah Ahmad Badawi as UMNO
president and Prime Minister without a general election. His government is
associated with the introduction of the **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** at 6%
in 2015, and with the founding in 2009 of **1Malaysia Development Berhad
([1MDB](/malaysia/1mdb-scandal))**, a state investment fund that later became the
entity at the centre of a large international financial scandal.

He led [Barisan Nasional](/malaysia/barisan-nasional) into the
**[2018 general election](/malaysia/2018-change-of-government)**, which the
coalition lost on **9 May 2018** to the opposition Pakatan Harapan — the first
change of federal government since independence. He left office the same period.

## Legal proceedings (public record)

As a matter of public record, Najib was **convicted in July 2020 in the SRC
International case**, concerning RM42 million from a former 1MDB unit that moved
into his accounts. The **Federal Court upheld the conviction and sentence on
23 August 2022**, and he began serving a prison term — the first former Malaysian
Prime Minister to be imprisoned. In **February 2024** the Federal Territories
Pardons Board reduced the sentence.

Further **1MDB-related proceedings** continued into 2025–2026, and both those
matters and a separate dispute over the terms of his custody were **under appeal**
at the time of writing. This article does not recount the detail of those
proceedings, which is carried by the court judgments and requires careful,
sourced treatment.

## Legacy

Najib remains a central and contested figure in modern Malaysian politics — his
premiership and the 1MDB affair are widely cited as turning points that reshaped
the party system and led to the [2018 change of government](/malaysia/2018-change-of-government).
