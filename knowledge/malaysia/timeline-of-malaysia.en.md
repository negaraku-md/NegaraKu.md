This timeline sets Malaysian history out on a single view — from the early
kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate, through centuries of colonial rule, to
independence, the formation of the modern federation, and the politics of
today. Each event is grouped by era so the overall shape of the story is clear
before the detail.

## Early kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| c. 1400 | Parameswara founds the Melaka Sultanate, which grows into a major Malay trading empire and a centre for the spread of Islam. |
| 1511 | Portuguese forces led by Afonso de Albuquerque take Melaka, beginning centuries of European presence. |

## The colonial era

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 1641 | The Dutch, allied with Johor, take Melaka from the Portuguese. |
| 1786 | Francis Light establishes a British trading settlement on Penang island for the East India Company. |
| 1824 | The Anglo-Dutch Treaty divides the region, placing the Malay peninsula within the British sphere of influence. |
| 1826 | Penang, Melaka and Singapore are combined into the Straits Settlements. |
| 1874 | The Pangkor Treaty introduces the British Residential system, extending colonial control over the Malay states. |
| 1896 | Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang form the Federated Malay States. |

## Occupation and the road to Merdeka

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 1941–45 | Japanese forces occupy Malaya and Borneo during the Second World War until their surrender in 1945. |
| 1946 | The Malayan Union proposal sparks wide opposition; UMNO is founded in response. |
| 1948 | The Federation of Malaya replaces the Malayan Union; the Emergency against a communist insurgency begins. |
| 1957 | The Federation of Malaya gains independence on 31 August 1957, with Tunku Abdul Rahman as its first Prime Minister. |

## Forming Malaysia

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 1963 | Malaysia is formed on 16 September 1963, uniting Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and (briefly) Singapore. |
| 1965 | Singapore leaves Malaysia and becomes an independent republic. |
| 1969 | Intercommunal violence erupts in Kuala Lumpur after the general election, leading to a declaration of emergency. |

## Modern Malaysia

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 1971 | The New Economic Policy launches to reduce poverty and restructure society in the years after 1969. |
| 1981 | Mahathir Mohamad becomes the fourth Prime Minister, beginning a 22-year term of rapid modernisation. |
| 1997–98 | The Asian financial crisis hits Malaysia; the government responds by imposing capital controls. |
| 2018 | The 14th general election produces the first change of federal government since independence. |
| 2022 | Anwar Ibrahim becomes the tenth Prime Minister, leading a unity government after a hung parliament. |
