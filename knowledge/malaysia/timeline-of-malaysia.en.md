This timeline marks the milestones of Malaysian history — from the rise of the
Melaka Sultanate around 1400, through the colonial era and the Japanese
Occupation, to Independence in 1957, the formation of Malaysia in 1963, and the
modern era. The page presents each event grouped by era.
