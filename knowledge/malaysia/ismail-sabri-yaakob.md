Ismail Sabri Yaakob (born 18 January 1960 in Temerloh, Pahang) was Malaysia's
**ninth Prime Minister**. A lawyer by training and a member of UMNO, he was
elected MP for Bera in 2004 and held a long series of federal cabinet
portfolios — youth and sports, domestic trade, agriculture, and rural
development — before serving in the security cluster and as Defence Minister,
where he fronted daily COVID-19 briefings.

## Rise to the premiership (2021)

Ismail Sabri was appointed **Deputy Prime Minister under
[Muhyiddin Yassin](/malaysia/muhyiddin-yassin) in July 2021**. Weeks later, when
Muhyiddin resigned on 16 August 2021 after losing his majority, the Yang
di-Pertuan Agong determined that Ismail Sabri commanded the support of a majority
of MPs. He was **sworn in as Prime Minister on 21 August 2021** — the third
person to hold the office within a single parliamentary term.

## Premiership (2021–2022)

In September 2021 his government signed a **Memorandum of Understanding on
"Transformation and Political Stability"** with the opposition Pakatan Harapan —
an arrangement credited with helping pass the anti-party-hopping law and lower
the voting age to 18 (Undi18), and with providing stability during the pandemic.

He **dissolved Parliament on 10 October 2022** to call the
**[2022 general election](/malaysia/2022-general-election)**, serving as
caretaker Prime Minister through the vote. His term ended when
[Anwar Ibrahim](/malaysia/anwar-ibrahim) was **sworn in as the tenth Prime
Minister on 24 November 2022**.

## After the premiership

Ismail Sabri remained the MP for Bera. As a matter of public record, he became
the subject of a **Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) investigation in
2025**, and a related court matter was **live at the time of writing** — there is
no conviction, and the matter is before the courts. This article does not recount
the detail of those proceedings, which must be reported only as far as current,
