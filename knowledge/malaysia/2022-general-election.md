Malaysia's **15th general election (GE15)** was held on **19 November 2022**,
after Prime Minister [Ismail Sabri Yaakob](/malaysia/ismail-sabri-yaakob)
dissolved Parliament on 10 October 2022. The main coalitions were **Pakatan
Harapan (PH)**, led by [Anwar Ibrahim](/malaysia/anwar-ibrahim); **Perikatan
Nasional (PN)**, led by [Muhyiddin Yassin](/malaysia/muhyiddin-yassin); and
**[Barisan Nasional](/malaysia/barisan-nasional) (BN)**.

## The first hung parliament

No coalition reached the **112 seats** needed for a majority in the 222-seat
Dewan Rakyat — a **first in Malaysian history**. Pakatan Harapan won about **82
seats**, Perikatan Nasional about **74**, and Barisan Nasional about **30**, with
the balance held by East Malaysian and other parties. **PAS**, a PN component,
emerged as the **single largest party by seats** — its best-ever result — as part
of a strong PN showing.

## A unity government

With no bloc able to form a government alone, the **Yang di-Pertuan Agong
(Al-Sultan Abdullah)** interviewed party leaders and urged the formation of a
unity government. **Anwar Ibrahim was sworn in as Malaysia's tenth Prime Minister
on 24 November 2022**, heading a "unity government" that brought together PH with
BN and other parties that had often been rivals.

GE15 was also the **first election held under the Undi18 reforms**, with the
voting age lowered from 21 to 18 and automatic voter registration — adding several
million new voters to the roll. Turnout was about 74%.
