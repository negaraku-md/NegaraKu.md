**1Malaysia Development Berhad (1MDB)** was a Malaysian government-owned
investment fund founded in 2009, during the premiership of Najib Razak, to
promote strategic economic development. It became the centre of one of the
world's largest financial scandals.

## What happened

According to the **U.S. Department of Justice**, more than **US$4.5 billion** was
misappropriated from 1MDB between 2009 and 2015 and moved through a network of
shell companies and bank accounts across multiple jurisdictions. The affair drew
investigations in the United States, Singapore, Switzerland and other countries,
and named the financier **Low Taek Jho ("Jho Low")** as a central figure; he
remains a fugitive.

Revelations from 2015 onward dominated Malaysian politics and contributed to the
defeat of the long-governing [Barisan Nasional](/malaysia/barisan-nasional)
coalition at the [2018 general election](/malaysia/2018-change-of-government).

## The legal cases

Multiple prosecutions followed in Malaysia and abroad. Former Prime Minister
**Najib Razak** was convicted in 2020 in the related **SRC International** case,
and in **December 2025** was convicted in the main 1MDB trial and sentenced to a
further term and fine. He has **filed appeals** against the convictions, so the
matter remains before the courts. Internationally, the **U.S. Department of
Justice** reported recovering or assisting in recovering about **US$1.1 billion**
in assets linked to the scheme, and the investment bank Goldman Sachs reached a
settlement over its role.
