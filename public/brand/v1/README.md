# NegaraKu.md logo — Version 1 (archived)

These are the **original v1 logo** assets: the black rounded hexagon with the
**abstract five-petal gold blossom** (stem + buds) in place of the "1".

They were replaced by **v2** — the hexagon with an exact vector trace of the
**Bunga Raya** (national hibiscus) national-flower emblem, recoloured to brand
gold. v2 is generated by `scripts/build-logo.mjs` (shape in
`scripts/logo-flower.mjs` / `src/lib/logo.ts`).

Kept here for reference and rollback. The SVGs are the source of truth; PNGs can
be regenerated from them. Colours: gold `#FFC000`, badge black `#0A0A0A`,
white rim `#FFFFFF`.

Files: `negaraku-icon(.svg/-red.svg)`, `negaraku-mark*`, `negaraku-lockup*`,
`negaraku-lockup-dark*`, and `favicon.svg`.
